Surat is a busy trading city, and many people who ask us about night cream wholesale here sell through Instagram, WhatsApp groups or their own shop rather than doing facials. If that is you, the real question is whether to buy loose cream and fill your own jars, or buy finished jars.

  • Finished jars are simpler. They arrive sealed and labelled, and you can start selling straight away.

Storing bulk cream in a humid city

  • Keep containers tightly closed in a cool, dry room, off the floor and away from wash areas.
  • Use a clean, dry spatula every time. Water getting into the cream is the most common reason bulk cream spoils early.
  • Store finished jars in their cartons, away from direct sun and damp walls.
  • In the monsoon, order smaller quantities more often.
