Hygiene tips for bulk cream in the salon

  1. Take cream out of the tub with a clean, dry spatula, never fingers.
  2. Put each client's cream in a fresh bowl, and never return leftovers to the tub.
  3. Keep the tub closed between clients and away from the steamer and sunlight.
  4. Patch test new clients on the jawline before a full facial, especially with kojic acid or glutathione cream.
  5. Avoid active creams straight after peels, waxing or threading on the same area.
  6. Write the opening date on the tub and finish older stock first.

Clients who are pregnant, or who have rashes, melasma or painful acne, should see a dermatologist before active facials.
