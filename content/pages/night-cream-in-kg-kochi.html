Two monsoons and year-round humidity

Kochi is warm and humid almost all year. The southwest monsoon brings heavy rain from around June to September, and the northeast monsoon adds more rain in October and November. For skincare, that means two things: clients usually want a light feel, and stored cream needs extra care against moisture.

  • In parlours, a thin layer of avocado night cream after a facial is usually enough. Heavier layers can feel sticky in this weather.
  • For clients with oily skin, the lighter avocado face cream is often the easier choice, and our guide on night cream for oily skin is worth sharing.
  • Any cream with kojic acid should be followed by sunscreen in the morning, even on cloudy monsoon days.

Storing bulk cream through the rains

  • Keep containers tightly closed in a dry room, off the floor and away from damp walls, steamers and wash basins.
  • Always use a clean, dry spatula. Water getting into the cream is the most common reason bulk cream spoils early, and in Kochi's air it happens easily.
  • Decant a day's supply into a small jar so the main container is opened less often.
  • During the monsoon months, order smaller quantities more often instead of holding stock for a long time.
