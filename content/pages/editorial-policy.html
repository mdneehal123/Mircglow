How we write our skincare guides

Our guides exist to help you make a good decision, even when that decision is not to buy from us. This page explains how they are written, checked and kept up to date.

Who writes them

Guides are written by Mohammed Nihal, who runs Mirc Glow. He is not a doctor, and the guides are general information, not medical advice. For skin conditions, allergies, pregnancy or anything that worries you, please see a dermatologist.

How we check facts

  • Claims about ingredients are checked against published research and ingredient references, not marketing copy.
  • When the evidence is weak or mixed, we say so in plain words.
  • Product details such as ingredients, sizes and prices come from our own labels and shop, and we update guides when they change.

What we will never claim

  • That a cream will permanently change your skin colour.
  • That a cosmetic cream treats or cures a medical condition.
  • Results in a fixed number of days. Skin changes slowly and differs from person to person.

Our products in our guides

We make and sell Mirc Glow creams, and our guides mention them. We always explain what kind of skin a product suits and who should be careful, and we include patch testing and sunscreen advice even when it slows results down.

Corrections and updates

We review guides regularly and when customers point out problems. If you find a mistake, message us on WhatsApp at +91 99808 81230 and we will fix it.
