A simple pre-wedding routine you can share

  1. Cleanse gently every evening with a mild face wash.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount of night cream and press it in.
  3. Use sunscreen every morning. It matters as much as the cream.
  4. Patch test any new cream at least two weeks before the wedding. Never start something new in the last week.
  5. Keep it simple. Do not add several new products close to the date.
