기본 · IN EVERY JAR

The base of every cream

Aloe vera

Light, fresh and hydrating. It balances richer ingredients so the cream does not feel heavy.

Vitamin B3

Also called niacinamide. Used for a smoother surface and a more even-looking tone, and well tolerated by most skin types.

Kojic acid

Used in routines for the look of dark spots and uneven tone. Wear sunscreen every morning when you use it.

자주 묻는 질문 · FAQ

Ingredient questions

What is glutathione?

An antioxidant your body makes naturally, used in creams designed for a brighter, more even-looking complexion. See glutathione cream benefits.

What is kojic acid?

A fermentation-derived ingredient used in formulas for a more radiant, even-looking tone. See kojic acid cream for dark spots.

What does avocado bring to skincare?

A rich, nourishing feel that helps skin feel soft, comfortable and cared for. See is avocado cream good for your skin.

Can I use these ingredients every day?

Yes, once your skin is used to them. Start with every second night for two weeks, and wear sunscreen in the morning.

Kojic acid or glutathione: which do I need?

Kojic acid is more targeted at the look of spots and tan; glutathione is gentler and aimed at overall radiance. We compare them in kojic acid vs glutathione.
