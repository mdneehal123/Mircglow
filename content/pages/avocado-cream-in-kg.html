How to store bulk avocado cream

Keep the tub tightly closed in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Always use a clean, dry spatula, never fingers, and never put unused cream back in the tub. Open one tub at a time and finish older stock first.

If you plan to repack or sell the cream under your own brand, the labelling and licence rules for cosmetics in India apply to you. Check them before you sell.
