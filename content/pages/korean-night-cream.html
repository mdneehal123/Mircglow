소개 · THE BASICS

What is a Korean night cream?

A Korean night cream is a moisturiser designed for the evening, built around the Korean approach to skincare. That approach, often called K-beauty, values hydration, gentle formulas and patient, consistent care over harsh quick fixes.

In a Korean routine, the night cream comes last. Lighter products go on first, and the cream sits on top to hold their moisture in. Because you are asleep and not wearing sunscreen or make-up, the cream can stay on the skin undisturbed for seven or eight hours.

The goal is the look that Korea made famous: glass skin. Glass skin means skin that appears smooth, even and so well hydrated that it reflects light. No cream creates it overnight. It comes from steady moisturising, night after night, and a good night cream is the product that does most of that work.

Compared with a traditional heavy cold cream, a Korean-style formula is usually smoother and less greasy. It is rich enough to comfort dry skin, yet it sinks in within a few minutes so it does not transfer to your pillow.

정직 · HONEST LABELS

Korean or Korean-inspired: what is the difference?

Many products sold in India use the word Korean on the label, and it can mean two different things. It helps to know which one you are buying.

Made in Korea means the product was manufactured in South Korea and imported. These products usually cost more because of import duty and shipping.

Korean-inspired means the product follows the K-beauty philosophy and ingredient style, but is made elsewhere. Mirc Glow creams are Korean-inspired. They are built around the same ideas, with hydrating textures, layering-friendly formulas and well-known ingredients such as vitamin B3, and they are priced for everyday use in India.

Neither is automatically better. What matters is the ingredient list, how the cream feels on your skin and whether you will use it every night. Always check the pack for the manufacturer, the batch number and the use-before date.

루틴 · THE NIGHT ROUTINE

How to use a Korean night cream: the 5-step evening routine

The order matters. Korean routines move from the thinnest texture to the thickest, and the cream always goes last.

  1. Oil cleanse. Massage a cleansing oil or balm over dry skin to dissolve sunscreen and make-up, then rinse.
  2. Water cleanse. Follow with a gentle foaming or gel cleanser. This two-part wash is called double cleansing.
  3. Tone. Pat a hydrating toner into damp skin with your hands.
  4. Treat. Apply an essence or serum if you use one, and let it settle for a minute.
  5. Seal. Warm a pea-sized amount of night cream between your fingertips and press it into your face and neck.

You do not need all five steps every night. On a tired evening, cleanse and apply your cream. Two steps done daily beat ten steps done once a week.

In the morning, rinse, moisturise lightly and finish with sunscreen. Daily sun protection matters even more when your night routine includes kojic acid. For more detail, read how to build a simple night skincare routine.

차이 · KNOW THE DIFFERENCE

Night cream, sleeping mask or day cream?

Night cream

Your everyday final step in the evening. It is rich, and it carries the ingredients you want working while you rest.

Sleeping mask

A Korean invention, also called a sleeping pack. It is a leave-on mask applied over or instead of your cream, usually two or three nights a week, for an extra layer of moisture.

Day cream

Lighter, so it sits well under sunscreen and make-up. A night cream is usually too rich for a humid Indian morning, which is why we suggest keeping it for the evening.

인도 · FOR INDIA

Using a Korean night cream in Indian weather

Korea has cold, dry winters, and many Korean creams are made with that climate in mind. India is different, so a few small adjustments help.

Summer and monsoon

Use a thinner layer. Cleanse well to remove sweat, sunscreen and pollution, then apply half your usual amount. Air conditioning dries the skin overnight, so a night cream is still worth using.

Winter

Apply a full pea-sized amount and include your neck. This is the season when a rich cream is most welcome, especially in North India.

Sun exposure

Indian sunlight is strong all year. Wear a broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning. No night cream can keep an even-looking tone if the skin is unprotected during the day.

3 mistakes to avoid

  • Applying too much. A thick layer does not work harder. It sits on the surface and feels sticky in humid weather.
  • Skipping the cleanse. Cream over unwashed skin traps the day's dust and oil.
  • Expecting overnight change. Give any new cream four to six weeks before you judge it.
