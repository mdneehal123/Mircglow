Shipping Policy

Here is how delivery works when you order from Mirc Glow.

The short version

  • Shipping is free.
  • Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 days.
  • Cash on Delivery and online payment are both available.

Where we deliver

We deliver across India. Before you order, send us your pincode on WhatsApp and we will confirm delivery to your area.

Shipping charges

Shipping is free on all orders. The price you see on the product page is the price you pay.

Delivery time

Most orders are delivered within 3 to 7 days of being placed. Delivery to remote areas can take a little longer. Delays can also happen during festivals, bad weather or courier disruptions, and we will keep you informed if your order is affected.

Payment options

  • Cash on Delivery. Pay in cash when your order arrives.
  • Online payment. Pay securely by UPI, card or net banking at checkout on our website (processed by Razorpay), or through a payment link if you order on WhatsApp.

Tracking your order

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive the tracking details by SMS, email or WhatsApp. If you have not received them, message us with your order number and we will share them. You can also use our track your order page.

If your parcel arrives damaged

Please do not accept a parcel that is visibly damaged or opened. If you discover damage after opening it, contact us within 7 days with a photo of the product and the outer package. See our returns and refunds policy for the full process.

Wrong address or missed delivery

Please check your address and phone number carefully when you order. If the courier cannot reach you, they will usually try again. If a parcel is returned to us because of an incorrect address or repeated missed deliveries, we will contact you to arrange the next step.
