Dry heat, dusty summers and the right cream

Hubli–Dharwad sits on the Deccan plateau, so it is drier than the coast. Summers from March to May are hot and dusty, the monsoon brings moderate rain, and winter mornings are pleasant but dry. Skin here tends to feel tight more often than it feels greasy, which changes what you should buy:

  • March to May and November to February: the richer avocado night cream suits most clients, especially anyone whose cheeks feel rough or flaky after a day outdoors.
  • June to September: the air is damper, so oily and combination skin may prefer avocado Korean night cream or the lighter avocado glow cream.
  • Any month: clients using a cream with kojic acid or glutathione should wear sunscreen the next day. Summer sun here is strong.

Storing large tubs

  • Keep tubs sealed and in the shade. A shelf near a sunny window or on a hot roof-facing wall can warm the cream in summer.
  • Use a clean, dry spatula every time; water or dirty tools are what spoil bulk cream early.
  • Decant a daily jar so the main tub is opened as little as possible.
  • Dust is a real nuisance in the dry months, so wipe the lid before opening.
