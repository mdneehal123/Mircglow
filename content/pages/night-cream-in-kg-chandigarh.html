Cold winters, dry air: why richer cream sells here

Chandigarh has properly cold winters. December and January bring chilly nights, foggy mornings and dry air, and many homes and offices run heaters, which dries skin further. Summers are hot, and the monsoon months are humid. In practice that means:

  • November to February: the richer avocado night cream is the better fit for most clients, especially for rough cheeks and flaky patches after a facial.
  • July to September: switch more stock to avocado Korean night cream or avocado glow cream, which feel lighter in humid weather.
  • Every season: clients using a cream with kojic acid or glutathione need sunscreen the next morning. Winter sun still counts.

Storing bulk cream in winter and summer

  • Keep tubs sealed in a room at normal temperature, away from room heaters and blowers in winter and direct sun in summer.
  • Cream can feel a little firmer on a cold morning. Let it sit at room temperature instead of warming it on a heater.
  • Always use a clean, dry spatula. Water or used tools getting into the tub is what spoils bulk cream early.
  • Fill a small daily jar so the main tub is opened less.
