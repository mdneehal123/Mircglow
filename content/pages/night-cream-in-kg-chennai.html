Storing bulk cream in a humid city

  • Keep containers tightly closed in a cool, dry room, away from steamers and wash basins.
  • Do not leave cream in a parked vehicle or near a window that gets afternoon sun.
  • Scoop out a day's supply into a small jar with a clean, dry spatula, so the main container is opened less often.
  • In the rainy months, order smaller quantities more often.
