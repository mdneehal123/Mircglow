Why start with kg, not thousands of jars

A new brand does not yet know which cream, texture or price will sell. Buying loose cream by weight lets you test with a small budget. If customers like it, you reorder. If not, you have not locked money into jars, boxes and labels.

A simple plan for a first order

  1. Pick one hero cream. Avocado night cream is the most requested, the Korean-inspired night cream suits most skin types, and glutathione cream is popular for radiance.
  2. Ask for a sample and test the texture, smell and feel yourself.
  3. Read the ingredient list. Make sure you are comfortable with every ingredient before you sell it.
  4. Decide jar sizes. 1 kg fills about 33 jars of 30 g, or 66 jars of 15 g.
  5. Design labels and claims honestly. Say the cream helps skin feel soft and look brighter. Do not claim it changes skin colour or treats a medical condition.
  6. Test with 20 to 50 customers and listen to what they say before you scale.

Questions to ask any supplier

  • Can I see the full ingredient list and the maker's details?
  • What is the minimum order and the price per kg at different quantities?
  • Do you provide a sample, and what is the dispatch time?
  • What are the shelf life and the storage advice?
  • Is there a batch number on every order?
