Keeping bulk cream safe in summer heat

Delhi summers can cross 40°C, and cream left in a hot storeroom can separate or change texture. Keep containers in the coolest room you have, away from windows and direct sun, and never in a parked vehicle. If you can, place summer orders so they arrive early in the week and do not sit over a weekend in transit.
