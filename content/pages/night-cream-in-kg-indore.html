Storing bulk cream in Indore

  • Keep containers tightly closed in a cool, shaded room. In April to June, keep them well away from direct sun and hot tin-roofed storerooms.
  • Never leave a parcel in a parked car or on a two-wheeler in the afternoon heat.
  • Use a clean, dry spatula and decant a day's supply into a small jar, so the main container is opened less often.
  • In the monsoon, keep stock off the floor and away from damp walls.
