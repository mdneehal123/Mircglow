What kojic acid does in a cream

Kojic acid is a well-known cosmetic ingredient, usually used at around 1–2% in creams. With regular use and daily sunscreen, it helps reduce the look of dark spots, tan and uneven tone. It works slowly. Skin renews roughly every 4–6 weeks, and a more even look usually takes 8–12 weeks.

It does not change natural skin colour, and it is not a quick fix. If you sell or use it, set those expectations honestly with your clients. Our guides on kojic acid cream for dark spots and kojic acid vs glutathione explain more, and are easy to share.

Safety tips for salons using kojic acid cream

  • Patch test every new client on the jawline or behind the ear before applying to the full face.
  • Do not layer it straight after a strong peel, waxing or threading on the same area.
  • Tell clients about sunscreen. SPF 30 or more every morning is essential with kojic acid.
  • Start slowly for home use. Clients with sensitive skin can begin every other night. Our kojic acid side effects guide covers what to watch for.
  • Refer when needed. Pregnant clients, or those with melasma, rashes or painful acne, should see a dermatologist first.

Storing and repacking bulk cream

Keep tubs closed, cool and out of sunlight. Use a clean, dry spatula, never fingers, and never put used cream back. Check the batch number and use-before date on each tub, and finish older stock first. If you repack or sell under your own name, cosmetic labelling and licence rules in India apply to you.
