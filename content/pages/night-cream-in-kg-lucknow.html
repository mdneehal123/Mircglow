One city, three very different seasons

Lucknow has hot summers, a humid monsoon from around July to September, and cold, foggy winters in December and January. Your clients' skin changes with each one, and so does the amount of cream they want.

  • Summer and monsoon: many clients prefer a thin layer. The lighter avocado face cream is often the easier daytime sell, worn under sunscreen.
  • Winter: heaters, cold air and hot-water baths leave skin feeling dry. This is when demand for a richer night cream usually rises.

Storing bulk cream through the year

  • Keep containers tightly closed in a cool, dry room, away from steamers, geysers and wash basins.
  • In May and June, keep stock out of direct sun and never leave it in a parked vehicle.
  • In the monsoon, use a clean, dry spatula every time. Moisture getting into the cream is the most common reason bulk cream spoils early.
  • Decant a day's supply into a small jar so the main container is opened less often.
