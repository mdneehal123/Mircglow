Storing bulk cream through a Nagpur summer

  • Heat is the bigger risk here than damp. Keep tubs in the coolest room you have, out of direct sun, and never in a vehicle or a tin-roofed store in May.
  • Do not keep the cream next to a cooler, where it can pick up moisture.
  • Use a clean, dry spatula and decant a day's supply into a small jar.
  • In April and May, buy what you will use in a few weeks rather than a big summer stock.
