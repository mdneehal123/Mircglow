Pune's three seasons, three kinds of skin

Pune is milder than Mumbai but changes a lot through the year, and clients' skin changes with it.

  • Summer (March to May). Hot and dry in the day. Clients often want lighter layers, so a thin coat of cream is enough.
  • Monsoon (June to September). Steady rain and damp air. Keep cream light and store stock carefully.
  • Winter (November to February). Cool, dry mornings. This is when avocado night cream tends to be most popular, because skin feels tight.

Storing bulk cream through the Pune monsoon

  • Keep containers tightly closed, in a dry room away from damp walls.
  • Use a clean, dry spatula every time. Water getting into the cream is the most common reason bulk cream spoils early.
  • Decant a day's supply into a small jar so the main container stays sealed.
  • In the rainy months, order smaller quantities more often.
