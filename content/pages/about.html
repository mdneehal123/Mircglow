약속 · OUR PROMISE

What we promise, and what we don't

  • We tell you what is in the jar. Key ingredients are listed on every product page, and the full list is printed on the pack.
  • We don't promise to change your skin colour. Our creams are cosmetics. They help skin feel moisturised and look smoother, brighter and more even. Anyone promising you a new skin tone is overselling.
  • We tell you to patch test and wear sunscreen. Even when it means a slower result, because it is the honest advice.
  • We make returns simple. Unopened, sealed jars can be returned within 7 days. Damaged, wrong or expired items are replaced or refunded. Details are in our returns policy.
