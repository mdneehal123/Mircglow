How to choose for many recipients

  1. Split your list into two or three groups, for example the whole team, managers, and top clients. One gift per group keeps the order simple.
  2. For the widest group, keep it simple. A single cream or the Day & Night Combo suits most people.
  3. If you do not know people's skin, pick the gentlest option. The Avocado Glow Cream 15 g has no kojic acid and works as a day or night moisturiser.
  4. Remember that most of our creams contain kojic acid. If you add your own card to each gift, two lines help: patch test before first use, and wear sunscreen in the morning.
  5. Men on the list? Our creams are everyday moisturisers that men use too; our night cream for men guide is easy to share.
