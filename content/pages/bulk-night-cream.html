What to check when you buy cream in bulk

  • Batch number and manufacturing date on every container.
  • The full ingredient list, so your own labels are accurate.
  • Use-before date, and how long the cream keeps once opened.
  • A sample first, to check texture, colour and fragrance.
  • Sealed, clean containers on delivery.

How to store bulk night cream

Keep containers tightly closed, in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Use a clean, dry spatula every time, and never return unused cream to the container. Open one container at a time and finish the oldest stock first.

가격 · PRICE PER KG

What decides the wholesale price per kg?

The night cream wholesale price depends on three things.

  • The cream. Formulas with more active ingredients, such as glutathione, cost more per kilo.
  • The quantity. Larger orders get a lower rate per kg.
  • The packing. Loose cream in bulk containers costs less than cream filled into finished jars.

That is why we quote each order individually. Send us your requirement and you will have a clear figure to compare.

Before you repack or rebrand

If you plan to fill the cream into your own jars or sell it under your own brand, the labelling and licence requirements for cosmetics apply to your business.
