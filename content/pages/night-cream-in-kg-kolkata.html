Storing bulk cream in Kolkata's humidity

  • Keep the containers shut tight, in a dry room and away from wash areas and steamers.
  • Take out what you need for the day into a small jar, so the main container is opened less often.
  • Use a clean, dry spatula. Water getting into the cream is the most common reason bulk cream spoils early.
  • In the monsoon, avoid holding large stock for months.
