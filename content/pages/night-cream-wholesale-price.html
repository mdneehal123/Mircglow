The three things that decide night cream wholesale price

1. The cream

Formulas with more active ingredients cost more to make. A simple avocado and aloe vera cream sits at the lower end. Avocado night cream with vitamin B3 and kojic acid costs more. Glutathione cream, with glutathione, vitamin C, kojic acid, aloe vera and vitamin B3, costs the most of our bulk creams.

2. The quantity

The more kilos you order, the lower the rate per kg. Mixing, filling and packing a larger batch costs less per kilo than handling a small one.

3. The packing

Loose cream in 1 kg tubs is the cheapest way to buy. Cream filled into finished jars costs more per gram, because jars, lids, labels and filling time are added.

How to compare wholesale quotes fairly

A low price per kg means little if the cream is not what you expected. When you compare suppliers, check:

  • The full ingredient list, so you compare like with like.
  • A sample, to check texture, colour and smell before you commit.
  • Batch number and manufacturing date on every container.
  • The use-before date, and how long the cream keeps once opened.
  • Delivery cost and time to your city.
  • Honest claims. Be wary of bulk creams sold with promises like overnight change or changing skin colour. Cosmetic creams cannot do that. Our guide to choosing a night cream in India explains what honest labels look like.

Working out your cost per jar

Once you have a price per kg, divide by the number of jars you will fill. One kilo fills about 100 jars of 10 g, 40 jars of 25 g, 33 jars of 30 g or 20 jars of 50 g. Allow a little extra for filling loss, then add your jar, label and packing cost. For a salon, a facial uses only a few grams, so one kilo covers a large number of clients.
