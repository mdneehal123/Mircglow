Jaipur's dry climate and your clients' skin

Jaipur has a dry climate. Summers are very hot with dusty winds, the monsoon is fairly short, and winters are cool and dry. Unlike coastal cities, the common complaint here is skin that feels tight, flaky or rough rather than oily.

  • After a facial, a slightly more generous layer of avocado night cream usually feels right, especially from November to February.
  • Clients who spend time outdoors in the dust and sun often like a cleanse, then night cream before bed. Our guide on avocado cream for dry skin is a useful link to share with them.
  • Any cream with kojic acid should be followed by sunscreen in the morning. In Jaipur's strong sun this is not optional.

Storing bulk cream in Jaipur's summer heat

  • Keep containers tightly closed in the coolest room you have, away from direct sun, windows and rooftop storerooms.
  • Never leave a parcel in a parked car or on a two-wheeler in the afternoon. Summer heat can change the texture of any cream.
  • Scoop a day's supply into a small jar with a clean, dry spatula, so the main container is opened less often and stays free of dust.
  • In May and June, order smaller quantities more often instead of holding large stock through the hottest weeks.
