Humid summers, foggy winters

Patna, on the banks of the Ganga, has hot and sticky summers, a humid monsoon from about late June to September, and cold winters when fog often settles over the city in December and January. That gives parlours two quite different seasons to buy for:

  • April to October: sweat and humidity make most clients want a light layer. Avocado Korean night cream or avocado glow cream is usually the better pick, and a thin layer is enough.
  • November to February: cold, dry air and room heaters leave skin tight and flaky. Stock more of the richer avocado night cream.
  • Remind clients to use sunscreen the morning after any cream with kojic acid or glutathione.

For clients who use bleach or waxing before functions, avocado glow cream is the gentler choice for the first day or two, since it has no kojic acid. Our monsoon skincare routine guide is good to share in the wet months.

Keeping bulk cream fresh in Patna

  • Keep tubs sealed in a dry, shaded room, away from wash basins and steamers, especially in the monsoon.
  • Use a clean, dry spatula every time. Water entering the tub is the most common reason bulk cream spoils early.
  • Decant a daily jar for the counter so the main tub stays shut.
  • In humid months, buy smaller quantities more often.
