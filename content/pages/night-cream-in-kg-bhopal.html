Storage tips for parlours

  • Store tubs sealed, out of direct sun and away from steamers and the wash area.
  • Scoop with a clean, dry spatula. Water or used tools in the tub are the most common cause of early spoilage.
  • Keep a small jar on the counter for the day and leave the main tub closed.
  • Write the date you opened each tub on the lid and use older tubs first.
