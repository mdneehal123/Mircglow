Why skincare makes a good Diwali gift

Sweets are finished in a day and decorations go back in the cupboard. A good cream is used every night for weeks. It is practical, it feels thoughtful, and it suits almost anyone.

  • It works for most people. Our creams suit women and men, and most skin types.
  • It is the right season. Diwali comes as the weather turns drier, which is when a nourishing night cream is most welcome.
  • It is easy to send. We deliver across India, so you can send a set straight to family in another city.

Order in time

Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 days. To be safe, place your order at least a week before Diwali. Shipping is free, and you can pay online or choose Cash on Delivery. If the gift is going to someone else's address, paying online is easier for them.

A note for the person receiving it

Most of our creams contain kojic acid, so it is worth passing on two simple tips with the gift: do a patch test before the first use, and wear sunscreen in the morning. You can also share our skincare routine builder, which gives a simple routine for every skin type.
