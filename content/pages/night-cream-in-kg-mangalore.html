Which cream suits Mangalore's weather?

Keeping bulk cream fresh in coastal damp

  • Store containers sealed, on a shelf rather than the floor, and away from damp walls, steamers and the wash basin.
  • Scoop with a clean, completely dry spatula. Moisture getting into the tub is the usual reason bulk cream spoils early.
  • Fill a small daily jar so the big container stays closed most of the day.
  • Between June and September, order smaller lots more often instead of keeping months of stock.
