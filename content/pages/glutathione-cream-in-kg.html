Using bulk glutathione cream safely with clients

  • Patch test on each new client, on the jawline or behind the ear, before a full facial.
  • Avoid using it straight after a strong peel, waxing or threading on the same area.
  • Remind clients to wear sunscreen every morning, because the cream contains kojic acid and vitamin C.
  • Explain realistic timelines: skin renews roughly every 4–6 weeks, and a more even look usually takes 8–12 weeks of regular use. Our guide on how long glutathione cream takes to work covers this.
  • Pregnant clients, or clients with melasma, rashes or painful acne, should see a dermatologist first.

Storage and repacking

Keep the tub closed in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Use a clean, dry spatula every time and never return unused cream to the tub. Check the batch number and use-before date on each container. If you repack or sell under your own brand, the labelling and licence rules for cosmetics in India apply to your business, so check them before you sell.
