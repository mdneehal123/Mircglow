What an avocado night cream can't do

We'd rather you buy with the right expectations. A night cream helps skin feel moisturised and soft, often from the first week. With steady nightly use and sunscreen every morning, the kojic acid and niacinamide jars can help skin look more even, but that takes time. Skin renews roughly every 4 to 6 weeks, so think 8 to 12 weeks, not days.

It won't change your natural skin colour. It isn't a fix for melasma, painful or cystic acne, eczema or rashes, and those deserve a dermatologist's visit. If you're pregnant, check with your doctor before using a cream with kojic acid.

Who should skip ordering right now: if your skin is currently irritated, peeling or reacting to something else, wait until it settles. If you just want something very gentle, the 15 g Glow Cream is the better buy over the kojic acid jars.
