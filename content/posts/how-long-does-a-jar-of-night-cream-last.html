How long does night cream last? Used once a night in a pea-sized amount, a 10 g jar lasts about 2 to 3 weeks, a 15 g jar about 3 to 4 weeks, a 25 g jar about 5 to 6 weeks and a 30 g jar about 6 to 8 weeks. Use it morning and night, or generously on the neck too, and those times roughly halve.

Why the figures allow for a little more than a pea

A pea-sized amount for the face is about 0.4 to 0.5 g. In real life, you also put a little on the neck, some stays on your fingertips, and the last bit in the jar is hard to scoop out. Together that comes closer to half to three-quarters of a gram a night, which is what the table above is based on.

If you use cream on your face only and scrape the jar clean, it can stretch a little longer. Our guide on how much night cream to use explains how to find the right amount for your skin type and season.

What makes a jar run out faster or slower

  • Morning and night use: roughly half the jar life.
  • Neck, chest and hands: each extra area adds up quickly.
  • Winter, AC or dry skin: a second thin layer on dry cheeks can shorten jar life by a week or more.
  • Oily skin in summer: half a pea is often enough, so the jar lasts longer.
  • Sharing: two people sharing one 30 g jar will finish it in about three to four weeks.
  • Skipping nights: the jar lasts longer, but the cream works through regular use, so this is not a saving.

A quick check that you are using the right amount

  1. Write the date on the lid the day you open a new jar.
  2. After two weeks, look inside. A 30 g jar should be roughly a quarter to a third used.
  3. If it is already half empty, you are using too much. Cut back to one pea for the face.
  4. If it has barely moved after a month, you are either missing nights or using too little. Skin that feels tight by morning confirms it.

How many jars until you see a difference?

Skin feels softer within days of starting any night cream. A more even-looking tone takes longer: skin renews roughly every 4 to 6 weeks, and most people need 8 to 12 weeks of nightly use with sunscreen every morning. That is one to two 30 g jars. Judge a cream at the end of the second jar, not halfway through the first.

Jar life vs shelf life

Finishing a jar in 6 to 8 weeks is also good for freshness. Once opened, every cream has a period after opening, shown as a small open-jar symbol such as 6M or 12M. Most people finish a jar well within that, but a half-used jar left in a drawer for months is a different story. Short answers

How long does a 30 g night cream last? About 6 to 8 weeks at a pea-sized amount once a night.

How long does a 10 g cream last? About 2 to 3 weeks of nightly use, which makes it a good size for patch testing.

Is a bigger jar better value? Usually. The 30 g Avocado Night Cream costs about ₹21.63 per gram, compared with ₹34.90 for the 10 g jar.

Why does my night cream finish so fast? Most often from using more than a pea, applying it twice a day, or adding the neck and hands from the same jar.

Should I use more cream to see results faster? No. A bigger amount does not speed anything up and can feel heavy or cause small bumps.

This is general skincare information, not medical advice. Patch test any new product, and see a dermatologist if you have a skin condition.
