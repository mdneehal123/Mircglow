What to check before buying any avocado night cream

  • Is the ingredient list shown? You should be able to read what is in a cream before you put it on your face. If it is not on the page, ask the seller or check the label on the jar.
  • Does it list kojic acid or other actives? Kojic acid can irritate some skin, so you should know if a cream has it and patch test first.
  • How fast does it promise results? Skin renews about every 4 to 6 weeks. A cream that promises a clear change in 10 days is promising more than most skincare can honestly deliver, so give any cream 8 to 12 weeks before judging it.
  • Does it claim to change your skin colour? No cosmetic changes natural skin tone. Look for wording such as softer, smoother and more even-looking.
  • Is the size right? A 30 g jar used in a pea-sized amount each night lasts about 6 to 8 weeks.

How to use either one safely

  1. Patch test behind the ear and wait 24 hours.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount on clean skin in the evening, as the last step.
  3. Start every other night, then go nightly if your skin is calm.
  4. Wear sunscreen every morning.
  5. Stop if you get lasting redness, burning or itching. Pregnant, breastfeeding or have a skin condition? Ask a doctor first.
