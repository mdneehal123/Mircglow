What the extra ₹1,000 gets you

Glass Glow Cream swaps vitamin C and kojic acid for three different ingredients:

  • Hyaluronic acid holds water in the skin for a plump, dewy look. This matters most if you sleep in AC, live in a dry North Indian winter, or your face feels tight by morning. More in hyaluronic acid night cream.
  • Centella asiatica, the "cica" plant from Korean skincare, is used for a calm, comfortable feel. See centella cream for Indian skin.
  • Alpha arbutin is the spot-focused ingredient here instead of kojic acid. It is known for being gentle and stable in a cream.

So you are paying for hydration and a calmer feel on top of the glow, not for "stronger" glutathione. If your skin is already comfortable and well moisturised, that extra may not be worth it for you.

What Glutathione Cream has that Glass Glow does not

  • Vitamin C, a well-known antioxidant used for a fresher, more radiant look on skin that sees a lot of sun, dust and pollution.
  • Kojic acid, one of the most widely used spot-focused ingredients, typically at about 1–2% in creams.
  • Aloe vera, for a light, soothing feel.

For many people with dull, tired-looking skin and no major dryness, this is all they need, at ₹1,000 less per jar. Our guide to glutathione cream benefits explains what glutathione can and cannot do.

Who should pick which

Pick Glutathione Cream if…

  • Your main concern is a dull, tired look after summer, travel or long hours.
  • Your skin is normal, combination or oily and does not feel dry at night.
  • You have used kojic acid before without tingling or redness.
  • You want to keep spend under about ₹1,100 a month.

Pick Glass Glow Cream if…

  • You want glow, hydration, smoother-looking texture and spot care from one jar instead of several serums.
  • Your skin feels tight in AC or in winter.
  • Kojic acid has stung or felt too strong for you before. Glass Glow has no kojic acid, so it may suit kojic-sensitive skin better, but patch test first, because any cream can bother some skin.
  • You have both dullness and visible marks, such as old pimple marks or sun patches.

Can you use both creams?

You can, but most people do not need to. Both contain glutathione and niacinamide, so layering them on the same night doubles up on those and stacks kojic acid with alpha arbutin. That is more active ingredients than your skin needs in one go, with little to gain.

If you already own both:

  1. Patch test each one separately, behind the ear or on the jawline, for 24 to 48 hours, a few days apart.
  2. Alternate nights: Glutathione Cream one night, Glass Glow Cream the next.
  3. Use a pea-sized amount of one cream only, on clean skin, as your last step.
  4. Wear broad-spectrum sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, every morning.
