Skin care for Patna weather means getting through a very hot summer with dry, dusty winds from about April to June, a humid monsoon from late June to September, and a cold, foggy winter from December to February that leaves skin tight and dry. Broad-spectrum sunscreen and a gentle face wash stay the same all year; your moisturiser and night cream go lighter in the hot and wet months and richer once the fog sets in.

Patna sits on the Ganga in the middle of the north Indian plains, far from the sea, so it swings from dry heat to sticky rain to real cold. One routine all year rarely feels right.

Patna summer: dry heat, hot winds and dust

From April to June, Patna days are very hot and the afternoon wind is dry and dusty. Skin tans fast, cheeks can feel rough even while the forehead sweats, and prickly heat shows up on the neck and back.

  • Stay out of the strongest sun around midday when you can. Use an umbrella, cap or dupatta outdoors.
  • Reapply sunscreen after lunch if you are out, and after heavy sweating.
  • When you get home, rinse dust and sweat off with plain water before it sits on your skin for hours.
  • Do not skip moisturiser because of the heat. Dry wind dehydrates skin, and dehydrated skin can look both dull and shiny.
  • Drink water through the day and take heat warnings seriously.

Monsoon in Patna: late June to September

The rains bring very humid air, and some localities see waterlogging. Expect greasiness, clogged pores, sweat bumps and itching in skin folds.

  • Use lighter textures and about half your usual night cream if your face feels greasy.
  • If you walk through dirty rainwater, wash your feet and legs with soap and clean water as soon as you can and dry between the toes.
  • Change damp clothes and socks quickly, and dry towels and pillow covers in the sun when it comes out.

Patna winter: fog, cold waves and heaters

December and January bring dense fog and cold-wave spells across Bihar. Hot baths, room heaters and woollens all dry out skin, so this is when Patna skin needs the most care. Right now, in late November, is a good time to switch to winter care.

  1. Keep bath water warm, not hot, and keep baths short.
  2. Pat skin half-dry and moisturise within three minutes, while it is still a little damp.
  3. Use a full pea-sized night cream on the cheeks and around the nose, and lip balm before sleep.
  4. Rub a thick cream into hands and heels at night; cracked heels are common in Patna winters.
  5. Keep sunscreen on. Winter sun after the fog lifts still tans, especially on rooftops.

Hard water, smoke and city dust

Many Patna homes use borewell or tank water, which can feel hard and leave skin tight after a wash. A gentle, non-foaming or low-foam face wash and a moisturiser straight after help; our hard water skincare guide explains more. On smoky, hazy evenings in October and November, and on dusty summer days, cleanse properly at night and avoid harsh scrubs. Our air pollution skincare guide covers this in detail.

Festivals and the ghats

Chhath Puja means long hours near water at dawn and dusk, and Makar Sankranti brings rooftop days. Apply sunscreen first, carry lip balm, and wash your face gently that night. After Holi, remove colour with a little oil or a gentle cleanser and plain water instead of scrubbing, and skip active creams for a night or two if your skin feels sore.

Patna vs Lucknow and Kolkata

Lucknow has a very similar plains climate with hot dusty summers and foggy winters, so our Lucknow weather guide is a good companion read. Kolkata is closer to the sea, so its monsoon is longer and stickier and its winter shorter and milder; see the Kolkata weather guide. Patna's big difference is how cold and dry its winter gets, so the winter steps above matter most here.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for sunburn with blisters, painful or cystic acne, prickly heat that becomes infected, an itchy rash in skin folds that keeps spreading in the monsoon, cracked skin that bleeds or will not heal, or patches of colour change that keep growing. If you feel dizzy or very unwell in the heat, get medical help straight away. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask your doctor before using creams with active ingredients.

Short answers

What is the best skin care routine for Patna weather? A gentle face wash, a moisturiser, broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning and a night cream. Go lighter from April to September and richer from November to February.

How do I protect my skin in Patna summer? Avoid midday sun when you can, cover up, reapply sunscreen after lunch, rinse off dust when you get home and keep using a light moisturiser.

Why does my skin get so dry in Patna winter? Cold, dry air plus hot baths and room heaters pull water out of the skin. Shorter warm baths, moisturiser on damp skin and a richer night cream help.

Do I need sunscreen on foggy days in Patna? Yes. Once the fog lifts, the winter sun still tans skin, so keep sunscreen in your morning routine.
