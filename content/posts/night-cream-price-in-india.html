Night cream price in India ranges from under ₹200 for basic moisturisers to several thousand rupees for imported or premium jars. For most people, a good everyday night cream costs somewhere between ₹300 and ₹1,500, and the most useful way to compare any two is the price per gram, not the sticker price.

This guide gives honest price bands by type, what you actually get at each level, how to work out cost per gram and per month, and our own prices so you can compare clearly.

A higher price does not always mean a better cream for you. It might pay for advertising, packaging or import costs rather than what is inside the jar.

How to work out price per gram

Divide the price by the number of grams. For example, ₹649 ÷ 30 g is about ₹21.6 per gram. Now you can compare a 10 g jar with a 50 g jar like for like.

Then think per month. A pea-sized amount for the face and a little for the neck is roughly 0.3–0.5 g per night, so most people use about 10–15 g a month. At ₹21.6 per gram, that is roughly ₹215–₹325 a month.

Check whether the size is in grams or millilitres. Creams are usually sold by weight; ml is close but not exact.

  • Full ingredient list. If you cannot see what is inside, do not buy.

  • Honest claims. Be careful with promises to change skin colour or show results overnight.

Is a more expensive night cream better?

Not automatically. The right cream is one that suits your skin type, feels good enough to use every night, and fits your budget for months, not weeks. A ₹649 cream used every night will usually do more than a ₹3,000 cream used twice a week. Our guide to the best night cream for glowing skin explains what to look for beyond price, and how to apply night cream helps a jar last longer.

Whatever you buy, patch test it for two to three nights first, and wear sunscreen every morning if it contains kojic acid, alpha arbutin or vitamin C.
