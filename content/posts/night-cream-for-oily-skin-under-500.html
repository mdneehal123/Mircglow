You do not need to spend a lot on a night cream for oily skin. What matters is the texture, a clear ingredient list and a maker you can trace. This guide shows what to check when your budget is ₹500 or less.

What to look for under ₹500

  • A light texture. Oily skin does best with a gel-cream or light cream that sinks in within a few minutes.
  • A full ingredient list. If a product hides what is in it, put it back.
  • A named manufacturer. Look for an address, a batch number and a use-before date.
  • Believable claims. A cosmetic can improve how skin looks and feels. It cannot transform skin in a week.

Do the per-gram maths

Jars come in every size, so compare the price per gram. Divide the price by the weight in grams. A 15 g jar at ₹399 costs about ₹27 per gram. A 50 g jar at ₹900 costs ₹18 per gram, but only works out cheaper if you finish it before the cream goes off. For a first try, a small jar is the smarter choice.

What to skip

  • Very thick creams or balms that feel waxy on oily skin.
  • Products with no batch number or maker address.
  • Anything promising "fair in 7 days".
  • Strong fragrance if your skin is easily irritated.

How to make a small jar last

  1. Use a pea-sized amount, not a thick layer.
  2. Apply to clean, slightly damp skin.
  3. Close the lid tightly and keep it away from steam and sunlight.
  4. Use a clean spatula or clean fingers each time.

Short answers

Is a cheap night cream good for oily skin? It can be, if the texture is light and the label is honest. Price alone does not tell you.

What should I check first? The ingredient list, the maker, the batch number and the use-before date.

How long does a 15 g jar last? With a pea-sized amount each night, usually a few weeks.

Can I use it every night? Most people can, after a patch test. Stop if your skin gets red, itchy or sore.

This is general skincare information, not medical advice. Patch test any new product, and see a dermatologist if you have a skin condition.
