A night cream under 1000 rupees can give you a full-size jar with a moisturising base and one or two well-known actives, which is all most skin needs at night. The useful way to choose is not the jar price but price per gram, texture for your skin type, and whether you want actives such as kojic acid or a plain moisturiser.

Below are the five options in our range under ₹1,000, a price-per-gram table, how to pick by skin type, and when it is genuinely worth spending more.

What changes between ₹500 and ₹1,000

  • Full sizes. Under ₹500 you mostly get small jars or very simple creams. Between ₹500 and ₹1,000, 25–50 g jars with a few useful ingredients become common.
  • Lower cost per month. A bigger jar used nightly usually works out cheaper than repeatedly buying small ones.
  • Room for a day and night pair. Some combos fit under ₹1,000.

What does not change: no cream at this price, or any price, should promise to change your natural skin colour. For the cheaper end, see our guide to night cream under 500.

How to choose by skin type

Dry skin, winter or AC rooms

Pick the 30 g Avocado Night Cream. It is the richer of our two avocado night creams and a good fit when skin feels tight after washing, which is common with hard water. See avocado cream for dry skin.

Oily skin or humid cities

Use the 15 g Avocado Glow Cream, or a thin layer of either night cream. In Mumbai, Chennai or Kochi humidity, less cream is usually more comfortable. Read night cream for oily skin.

Combination or normal skin

The 25 g Avocado Korean Night Cream suits most skin types. Use a little more on dry cheeks and less on the T-zone.

Sensitive skin

Start with the 15 g Avocado Glow Cream, which has no kojic acid. Move to an active cream later, after a patch test, if you want one.

Dull or uneven-looking skin

Either 25 g or 30 g cream, used nightly with sunscreen every morning. Expect 8–12 weeks for a more even look, not days.

When it is worth paying more

Before you buy online

  • Buy from the brand's own site or a trusted seller, and check the seal on arrival.
  • Look for the use-before date and batch number on the pack.
  • Patch test for 24–48 hours on the jawline before full use.
