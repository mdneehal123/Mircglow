How Often Should You Apply It?

If your skin is already comfortable with the formula, nightly use can be a simple way to keep your evening routine consistent. Apply it after cleansing and any lightweight leave-on steps, using only the amount needed to leave skin feeling moisturised rather than overloaded.

If you are trying a new product, begin every other night for the first week. This gives you time to notice whether the texture and finish suit your skin. If your skin feels comfortable, you can move to nightly use.

What Makes a Night Cream Feel Right?

  • Texture: Choose a finish that feels nourishing without feeling heavy for your preferences.
  • Routine fit: Keep the rest of your evening routine simple when introducing a richer cream.
  • Consistency: A product you enjoy using regularly is often more useful than an overly complicated routine.

When to Use It Less Often

Scale back if your skin feels uncomfortable, unusually dry, or overwhelmed after use. Consider simplifying the rest of your routine, especially when adding a new exfoliating or active product. Patch testing a new formula and following its label directions are sensible first steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use avocado night cream every night?

Many people can, provided the formula feels comfortable and is used as directed. Start gradually if it is new to your routine.

Should I apply night cream on damp skin?

Applying cream after cleansing while skin is still slightly damp can feel comfortable for many routines. Avoid applying to dripping-wet skin, and follow the product directions.

Can I layer a serum under night cream?

Yes. Apply lighter products first, then use night cream as your final moisturising step. Introduce new products one at a time when possible.
