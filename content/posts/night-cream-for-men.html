For a long time, skincare for most Indian men meant soap and whatever cream happened to be in the house. That is changing quickly, and night cream for men is one of the things people now search for most.

It makes sense. Sun, dust, shaving and long hours outdoors leave skin dry and uneven-looking, and a night cream is the least effort for the most return: one product, once a day, while you sleep. This guide is short on purpose.

Do men need a different cream?

Not really. Skin wants the same things whoever it belongs to: moisture, gentle handling and protection from the sun. A few practical points are worth knowing, though.

  • Men's skin is often oilier. A thin layer usually does the job.
  • Shaving is rough on skin. A comforting cream at night helps it feel calmer.
  • Many men skip sunscreen. Time in the sun without it shows up as tan and patchy tone.

So you do not need a jar that says "for men" on it. You need a cream that suits your skin type.

What a night cream does

  • Puts back the moisture your skin lost during the day, so it feels soft and not tight.
  • Helps skin look smoother and less dull in the morning.
  • With vitamin B3 or kojic acid, supports a more even-looking tone over several weeks.

How to pick one

  1. Work out your skin type. Shiny by afternoon is oily. Tight after washing is dry. Shiny only on the forehead and nose is combination.
  2. Match the ingredients to your goal. Niacinamide for a smooth look. Kojic acid for the look of tan and dark spots. Avocado for dry skin. Glutathione for an overall fresh look.
  3. Read the label. You want a full ingredient list, a named manufacturer and a use-before date.
  4. Keep it to one. One good cream beats five you never open.

The two-minute routine

  1. Wash your face with a face wash. Not soap.
  2. Pat it dry. Do not rub with the towel.
  3. Apply the cream. A pea-sized amount for the face and neck, or half that if you are oily.
  4. Do not skip the beard area. Work a little into the skin under your stubble or beard.
  5. Sleep. That is it.

In the morning, wash your face and put on sunscreen. If you are only going to do one thing all day, do that.

If you shave at night

Wait a few minutes after shaving before you apply cream, and keep it off any nicks. If your skin is stinging, skip creams with kojic acid for that night and go back to them the next day.

How long before it shows?

Your skin will feel softer after one night. A smoother, more even look takes six to eight weeks of using it every night, with sunscreen in the day.

Common mistakes

  • Soap on the face. It dries the skin out.
  • Too much cream.
  • No sunscreen.
  • Stopping after a week.
