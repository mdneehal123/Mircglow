To build a simple skincare routine, start with just three steps: cleanse, moisturise and use sunscreen every morning, then cleanse and moisturise at night. That is a complete skin care routine for beginners. Once your skin is happy with those basics for 3–4 weeks, you can add one extra product, such as a serum or a brightening cream, if you have a specific goal.

Most people quit skincare because they start with too many products at once. This guide shows how to build a simple skincare routine step by step, how to pick each product, and how to add more without confusing your skin.

Step 1: know your skin type

Your skin type decides textures. Oily skin likes gels, dry skin likes creams, combination skin likes light creams used more on the cheeks. Sensitive skin needs short, fragrance-free ingredient lists. If you are unsure, take our quick skin quiz.

A simple morning routine for beginners

  1. Cleanse. A mild face wash for 30 seconds with lukewarm water. Pat dry with a clean towel.
  2. Moisturise. A pea-sized amount, warmed between your fingers and pressed in.
  3. Sunscreen. Wait a minute, then apply SPF 30 or higher. Reapply every 2–3 hours if you are outdoors.

Total time: about 3 minutes. Our avocado day cream guide shows how to keep the morning layer light under sunscreen.

A simple night routine for beginners

  1. Take off the day. If you wore sunscreen or makeup, use micellar water or a cleansing balm first.
  2. Cleanse. Your usual face wash.
  3. Moisturise or night cream. A thin, even layer. Night is when you can use a slightly richer cream or one with actives.

Face cream for daily use: what to look for online

When buying a face cream for daily use online, you can't touch the texture, so read carefully:

  • Full ingredient list on the product page, not just two hero ingredients.
  • Texture description (gel, light cream, rich cream) that matches your skin type.
  • Clear directions for day or night use, and whether sunscreen is needed.
  • Small sizes for first-time buyers, so you can patch test.
  • Return and shipping policies you can actually read.
  • Honest wording. Skip creams that promise to change your skin colour.

Brightening face cream for everyday use

If your main goal is a brighter-looking, more even complexion, a brightening face cream for everyday use can be your fourth step, once the basics are settled. Common ingredients include niacinamide, kojic acid, vitamin C, alpha arbutin and glutathione. A few rules:

  • Use creams with kojic acid or other brightening actives at night, or in the day only under sunscreen.
  • Start 3–4 nights a week, then every night if your skin is comfortable.
  • Don't stack several brightening products at once.
  • Expect 8–12 weeks of steady use, because skin renews roughly every 4–6 weeks.

How to add new products without problems

  1. One at a time. Add one product, wait 2 weeks, then decide on the next.
  2. Patch test. A small amount behind the ear or on the jawline for 3–5 days.
  3. Thin to thick. Apply watery products first (toner, serum), creams after, sunscreen last in the morning.
  4. Keep notes. Write down what you started and when, so you know what helped or what didn't.

If you add a serum later, see how to layer serum and night cream.

Beginner mistakes to avoid

  • Buying a 10-step kit on day one.
  • Washing your face too often or with bathing soap.
  • Skipping sunscreen on cloudy days or indoors near windows.
  • Using DIY lemon, toothpaste or baking soda on the face.
  • Switching products every week before anything has time to work.
  • Expecting a cream to change your natural skin colour.

Short answers

What is the simplest skincare routine? Cleanser, moisturiser and sunscreen in the morning, and cleanser plus moisturiser at night.

How many products does a beginner need? Three: a face wash, a moisturiser and a sunscreen. Add more only if you have a clear goal.

Can I use the same cream day and night? Yes, if it is light enough for day and you wear sunscreen over it.

When should I add a brightening cream? After 3–4 weeks of steady basics, used at night with sunscreen every morning.

How long before I see a difference? Skin usually feels better within days; a brighter, more even look takes about 8–12 weeks.

This is general skincare information, not medical advice. Patch test any new product, and see a dermatologist if you have a skin condition.
