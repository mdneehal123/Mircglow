Skin care for Ranchi weather means handling a hot, bright summer with stormy afternoons from about March to May, a long, wet monsoon from June to September, and a cool, dry winter from November to February with properly cold nights. Broad-spectrum sunscreen and a gentle face wash stay the same all year; your moisturiser and night cream go lighter in the rains and richer when the winter air turns dry.

Ranchi sits on the Chota Nagpur plateau, a few hundred metres higher than the Gangetic plains. That height gives it milder summer nights than Patna or Kolkata, but also chilly winter mornings and very dry air from December, which is when most people notice their skin.

A simple daily routine for Ranchi skin

Morning

  1. Gentle face wash with lukewarm water. On cold mornings, resist very hot water on your face.
  2. Moisturiser: a thin gel or lotion in the monsoon, a creamier one from November to February. Our Avocado Glow Cream 15 g (₹399) has only avocado and aloe vera and no kojic acid, so it works as a plain day moisturiser.
  3. Broad-spectrum sunscreen SPF 30 or higher, about two finger-lengths for face and neck. Plateau sun is strong even when the air feels cool.

Night

  1. Cleanse to remove sunscreen, sweat and dust.
  2. Night cream: about half a pea-sized amount on humid monsoon nights, a full pea-sized amount from November to February. Our Avocado Night Cream (30 g, ₹649) has avocado, aloe vera, vitamin B3 and kojic acid; if you use it, wear sunscreen every morning.
  3. Lip balm before sleep in the dry months.

Ranchi summer: bright sun and stormy afternoons

Ranchi summers are hot rather than extreme on most days, but the sun is bright and the air is dry, so skin tans quickly and cheeks dry out. Some afternoons bring sudden thunderstorms with wind and dust before the rain.

  • Avoid the strongest sun around midday when you can, and use a cap, umbrella or dupatta outdoors.
  • Reapply sunscreen after lunch if you are out, and after sweating.
  • Rinse off dust after a windy, stormy afternoon instead of going to bed with it.
  • Keep a light moisturiser in the routine; dry air dehydrates skin even when the forehead looks shiny.
  • Drink water through the day.

Monsoon in Ranchi: June to September

Jharkhand gets a long, wet monsoon. Days are cooler, but the air stays damp, clothes take time to dry and skin can feel congested.

  • Switch to lighter textures and about half your usual night cream if your face feels greasy.
  • Dry your face, neck, underarms and between the toes fully after getting wet.
  • Change damp clothes and socks as soon as you get home, and dry towels in the sun when it comes out.
  • Keep sunscreen on cloudy days. UV still reaches the skin through cloud.

Waterfall and hill trips: Hundru, Dassam, Jonha and Netarhat

Monsoon and post-monsoon weekends often mean a drive to the waterfalls near Ranchi, and winter means trips to Netarhat for the sunrise. Both are long days outdoors.

  1. Apply sunscreen before you leave and reapply every two hours. Spray and sweat wash it off faster.
  2. Carry a cap, a small moisturiser and lip balm.
  3. After getting wet at the falls, dry your face and change into dry clothes as soon as you can.
  4. For an early Netarhat sunrise, moisturise and use lip balm before you step into the cold wind.
  5. Wash your face gently that night and use your usual night cream; skip new products on trip days.

Ranchi winter: cold nights and dry air

From late November to February, Ranchi nights can get properly cold while the days stay sunny. The air is at its driest, and hot baths and heaters make it worse.

  1. Keep bath water warm, not hot, and baths short.
  2. Moisturise within three minutes of washing, while skin is still a little damp.
  3. Use a full pea-sized night cream on the cheeks and around the nose, and lip balm before sleep.
  4. Rub a thick cream into hands and heels at night.
  5. Do not sit very close to a heater; our room heater skin care guide explains why.
  6. Keep sunscreen on; the midday winter sun on the plateau still tans skin.

Ranchi vs Patna and Kolkata

Patna, down on the plains, has a hotter, dustier summer and a foggier winter; see our Patna weather guide. Kolkata is humid for much longer and barely gets a real winter; see the Kolkata weather guide. Ranchi's big difference is its dry, cold winter nights with strong daytime sun, so moisturiser plus sunscreen matter most here from November.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for sunburn with blisters, painful or cystic acne, an itchy rash in skin folds that keeps spreading in the monsoon, cracked skin that bleeds or will not heal, or patches of colour change that keep growing. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask your doctor before using creams with active ingredients.

Short answers

What is the best skin care routine for Ranchi weather? A gentle face wash, a moisturiser, broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning and a night cream. Go lighter from June to September and richer from November to February.

Why does my skin feel dry in Ranchi winter? The air on the plateau gets very dry and cold at night, and hot baths make it worse. Moisturise on damp skin and use a fuller night cream.

Do I need sunscreen in Ranchi in winter? Yes. Winter days are clear and the midday sun is strong, so sunscreen stays in the morning routine all year.
