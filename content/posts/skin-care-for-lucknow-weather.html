Skin care for Lucknow weather means planning for two opposite seasons: a very hot summer with dry, dusty winds, and a cold, foggy winter that leaves skin tight and flaky. A gentle face wash, a moisturiser that changes with the season, broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning and a pea-sized night cream suit most skin in Lucknow.

In between come a humid monsoon and a short, pleasant spring and autumn. Lucknow sits on the plains of north India, so it also gets the hazy, smoggy weeks of early winter. This guide gives you one simple routine and shows what to change in each season, without adding a long list of products.

The pattern is simple: lighter textures from April to September, richer care from November to February. You do not need two full sets of products; often the same cream works if you change how much you use.

A simple daily routine for Lucknow skin

Morning

  1. Gentle face wash with lukewarm water. In winter, plain water in the morning is enough for dry skin.
  2. Moisturiser: a thin layer in summer and the monsoon, a slightly more generous one in winter. Our Avocado Glow Cream 15 g (₹399) has only avocado and aloe vera and no kojic acid, so it also works as a plain daytime moisturiser.
  3. Broad-spectrum sunscreen SPF 30 or higher, about two finger-lengths for face and neck. Keep it on during foggy winter days too, because daylight still reaches your skin.

Night

  1. Cleanse to wash off sunscreen, sweat and the day's dust or smoke.
  2. Night cream: about half a pea-sized amount in the monsoon if your skin is oily, a full pea-sized amount from November to February. Our Avocado Night Cream (30 g, ₹649) has avocado, aloe vera, vitamin B3 and kojic acid; if you use it, wear sunscreen every morning.
  3. Lip balm before bed in winter.

Lucknow summer: loo, dust and strong sun

From April to June, hot winds dry out the surface of the skin while sweat makes the forehead and nose shiny. Many people feel oily and tight at the same time.

  • Cover your face and arms with a cotton scarf or dupatta when you are out in the afternoon, especially on a two-wheeler. Our skincare for bike riders guide has more on this.
  • Reapply sunscreen after lunch and after wiping sweat.
  • Rinse dust off with cool water when you get home; you do not need a scrub.
  • Drink enough water through the day. It will not change your skin overnight, but heat and loo winds dehydrate the whole body.
  • Use a thin layer of cream at night; heavy layers feel sticky in the heat.

Lucknow monsoon: humidity and damp clothes

From July to September, the air stays humid between showers. Skin feels oily, and damp clothes or towels can lead to itchy skin folds.

  • Change out of damp clothes soon after coming in from the rain.
  • Dry your face, neck and skin folds fully. Pat, do not rub.
  • Keep the night cream thin and focus on the cheeks if your T-zone gets oily.

Lucknow winter: fog, heaters and dry skin

December and January bring cold, foggy mornings. Many homes use room heaters or blowers, and long hot baths feel tempting. Both dry out skin quickly.

  • Bathe in warm, not very hot, water and keep it short.
  • Apply moisturiser within a few minutes of washing, while the skin is still slightly damp.
  • Keep a bowl of water in the room if you use a heater for many hours, and do not sit right in front of it.
  • Use a richer layer of night cream on dry cheeks, around the nose and on the chin.
  • Do not stop sunscreen just because the sun is weak; winter afternoons on the terrace still count.

Smoggy weeks and hard water

From late October into winter, Lucknow often has hazy, smoky days, as many cities on the north Indian plains do. A gentle cleanse every evening is more useful than extra products; see our skincare for air pollution guide.

Many homes also use borewell water. If your water leaves white marks on taps and buckets, it is probably hard, and it can leave skin feeling tight after a wash. Our hard water skincare guide has simple fixes.

Lucknow vs Delhi weather

Both cities have hot summers, a monsoon and cold, smoggy winters, so the routine is similar. Lucknow is usually a little more humid than Delhi, so lighter textures may suit you for longer in the year. If you travel between them, see our Delhi weather guide.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for cracked, bleeding skin that does not get better, itchy rashes that spread, painful or cystic acne, patches of colour change that keep growing, or any rash in pregnancy. A face cream is not the answer for these.

Short answers

What is the best skin care routine for Lucknow weather? A gentle face wash, a moisturiser, broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning and a pea-sized night cream. Use lighter textures in summer and the monsoon and richer care in winter.

Do I need sunscreen in Lucknow winter? Yes. Daylight reaches your skin even on foggy days, so use SPF 30 or higher every morning.

Why does my skin feel dry in Lucknow winter? Cold, dry air, room heaters and hot baths all take moisture from the skin. Moisturise on damp skin and keep baths warm, not hot.

Can I use the same night cream all year? Often yes. Use about half a pea-sized amount in the humid months and a full pea-sized amount in winter.

How do I protect my skin from loo in Lucknow? Cover your face and arms outdoors, reapply sunscreen, rinse off dust with cool water and keep night care light.

This is general skincare information, not medical advice. Patch test any new product, and see a dermatologist if you have a skin condition.
