Skin care for Vijayawada weather means sun protection, a very light gel moisturiser and fewer layers for most of the year, because the city is hot, and often humid, from March to October. From December to February, mornings are milder and drier, so you can switch to a slightly creamier moisturiser and a full night cream, while a gentle face wash and broad-spectrum sunscreen stay the same every day.

Vijayawada sits inland on the Krishna river, ringed by rocky hills, so it misses the sea breeze that cools Visakhapatnam and gets some of the hottest summer days in Andhra Pradesh. The main skin challenges are strong sun, sweat and oil in the heat, sticky monsoon months and long hours outdoors at temples, markets and on two-wheelers.

Vijayawada summer: extreme heat and strong sun

From March to early June, afternoons can be fierce and nights stay warm, so skin rarely gets a break. Sweat mixes with sunscreen and dust, and many people end up over-washing.

  • Plan errands and outdoor work for early morning or after sunset where you can, and cover with a cap, umbrella, dupatta or light full sleeves.
  • Reapply sunscreen after lunch if you are outdoors, and after heavy sweating. Blot sweat with a soft cloth before you reapply.
  • Use face wash twice a day at most. In between, rinse with plain water.
  • Skip heavy creams and oils in the day. One thin layer of gel moisturiser is enough.
  • Wear loose cotton and take a cool shower at night to calm prickly heat. Drink water through the day.

Monsoon and cyclone season

Vijayawada gets rain from the south-west monsoon in June to September and more from depressions and cyclones over the Bay of Bengal in October and November. The air stays warm and humid for months.

  • Keep the routine short: face wash, gel moisturiser and sunscreen in the morning; cleanse and a little night cream at bedtime.
  • Do not skip sunscreen on cloudy days; UV still gets through.
  • After walking through flooded or dirty water, wash your feet and legs with soap and clean water, dry well and change into dry footwear. See a doctor for any cut or wound that came into contact with flood water.
  • Dry the neck, underarms and the gaps between toes fully, and wash face towels and pillow covers often.

Winter in Vijayawada: mild and drier

December to February is the most comfortable time of year, with cool early mornings and lower humidity. Skin that felt oily all summer may now feel tight on the cheeks.

  • Move to a slightly creamier moisturiser and a full pea-sized night cream.
  • Keep bath water warm, not very hot.
  • Use lip balm and a thick cream on heels at night.
  • Keep sunscreen on: winter days are still bright and sunny here.

Temple visits, Krishna river days and festivals

  1. Kanaka Durga temple on Indrakeeladri hill: queues and the climb can mean long hours in sun. Apply sunscreen before you go, carry a cap or umbrella, and walk barefoot sections early in the day when stone is cooler.
  2. Dasara (Navaratri) crowds: long days and late nights. Keep makeup light, carry blotting paper and remove everything before you sleep.
  3. Krishna river, Prakasam Barrage and Bhavani Island outings: water reflects sunlight, so reapply sunscreen every two hours and wear a cap.
  4. After a river dip: rinse with clean water when you can, pat dry and moisturise.

Hard water, AC and dust

Water in many homes can be hard and leave skin feeling tight; a low-foam face wash and moisturiser straight afterwards help, as our hard water skincare guide explains. Long hours in AC shops and offices dry skin even in summer, so keep a light moisturiser handy; see night cream in an AC room.

Vijayawada vs Visakhapatnam and Hyderabad

Visakhapatnam has a similar monsoon and cyclone season but a sea breeze and salty air; see our Visakhapatnam weather guide. Hyderabad is drier and cooler in winter, on the Deccan plateau; if you travel there often, see the Hyderabad weather guide.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for sunburn with blisters, painful or cystic acne, a spreading itchy rash in skin folds or between the toes, heat rash that becomes painful or infected, or patches of colour change that keep spreading. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask your doctor before using creams with active ingredients.

Short answers

What is the best skin care routine for Vijayawada weather? A gentle face wash, a thin gel moisturiser and broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning, and a small amount of night cream at bedtime. Use a little more cream only in the drier December to February months.

How do I stop my face getting oily in Vijayawada summer? Use face wash twice a day, a gel moisturiser, a matte sunscreen and blotting paper. Over-washing and skipping moisturiser often make skin oilier.

Do I need night cream in Vijayawada? Only a little for most of the year, mainly on cheeks or dry areas. From December to February, a full pea-sized amount helps.

How do I protect my skin at the Durga temple queue? Apply sunscreen before you leave, carry a cap or umbrella and water, and go early in the day when you can.
