Texture, absorption and smell

Texture. It is a creamy night cream, but the buyers above describe it as lightweight rather than heavy. A pea-sized amount covers the face.

Absorption. Two buyers mention that it absorbs quickly. Press it in gently and give it a few minutes before your face touches the pillow.

Smell. The cream has a noticeable scent. Some people do not mind it, but one buyer found it very strong. If you are unsure, patch test first and see how you feel about the smell over a few nights.

How it feels in Indian weather

  • Summer and humidity. Use a thin layer. More cream will not work faster and can feel heavy in the heat.
  • Monsoon. A thin layer is still enough. Cleanse well before applying, since sweat and pollution build up during the day.
  • Winter. Use a full pea-sized amount, and add a little on the neck.
  • AC rooms. Cooled air dries skin overnight, so keep using it all year if you sleep with the AC on.

Who it suits, and who should be careful

It suits most adults, women and men, who want a simple night moisturiser and a more even-looking tone over time. Dry and normal skin usually enjoy it most. Oily skin can use it too, in a thin layer focused on the cheeks.

Be careful if you have very sensitive skin, a fragrance sensitivity, or skin that reacts to active ingredients. Start every second night for two weeks. Read avocado night cream side effects to know what to watch for, and see a dermatologist first if you are pregnant or have melasma, rashes or painful acne.

Realistic results timeline

  1. First few nights. Skin usually feels softer and less tight in the morning.
  2. First 2 weeks. Dry patches tend to settle and skin looks more hydrated.
  3. 8 to 12 weeks. With nightly use and sunscreen every morning, tone can look more even. Skin renews roughly every 4 to 6 weeks, so this takes time.

It does not change your natural skin colour. It helps skin look healthier and more even with steady use.
