Skin care for Pune weather means planning for two very different halves of the year: a long, damp monsoon when skin turns oily and congested, and dry months when cheeks feel tight and the sun is strong. One simple routine works all year if you change only the amount of cream and how often you reapply sunscreen.

Pune sits on the Deccan plateau, higher and drier than Mumbai, so it is milder than the coast but has bigger swings between day and night. Add two-wheeler commutes, IT-park AC, students living in hostels and PGs, and weekend treks, and skin needs slightly different care from season to season.

A simple daily routine for Pune skin

Morning

  1. Gentle face wash with cool or lukewarm water. In winter, a plain water rinse is often enough for dry skin.

  3. Broad-spectrum sunscreen SPF 30 or higher, about two finger-lengths for face and neck, every day.

Night

  1. Cleanse well to remove sweat, dust and sunscreen. Cleanse twice if you wore makeup.

Pune monsoon: oily but not always hydrated

Pune's monsoon is less intense than Mumbai's but lasts a long time, and the air stays damp for weeks. Many people get oilier skin, clogged pores and small bumps on the forehead and chin.

  • Use less product, not stronger product. Skip heavy layers and thick oils.
  • After getting caught in the rain, rinse your face with clean water, pat dry and reapply a thin moisturiser.
  • Change pillow covers and face towels often; damp fabric does not dry well in this season.
  • Do not scrub every day to "fix" oiliness. It usually makes skin redder and oilier.

Pune winter: cool nights and dry air

From about November to February, Pune mornings and nights can feel properly cold while afternoons stay bright. The big gap between day and night is what dries skin out.

  • Bathe with lukewarm water. Very hot showers on a cold morning strip skin and leave it itchy.
  • Moisturise face and body within a minute or two of bathing.
  • Keep sunscreen on. Clear winter skies still bring strong sun by noon.
  • Carry a lip balm; cracked lips are common in this season.

Two-wheelers, traffic and dust

Many Puneites ride to college or work, often in heavy traffic and roadwork dust. Sun, sweat and dust together leave skin dull-looking and tanned on the cheeks, nose and neck.

  • Apply sunscreen before the ride and cover your face with a clean cotton scarf.
  • Wash the scarf and helmet pads often; dirty fabric can cause bumps along the jaw and forehead.
  • If your face feels gritty when you arrive, rinse with plain water and moisturise lightly.

IT parks, hostels and treks

Long AC hours

A hot ride followed by a full day in cold AC dries skin by evening. A small desk kit helps: a light moisturiser for dry areas, sunscreen for the ride home, lip balm and a water bottle. Our AC dry skin routine explains the details.

Students in hostels and PGs

Pune has a large student population, and shared bathrooms often mean tanker or borewell water that can be hard. Keep your routine to three products, use your own towel, and moisturise right after washing. Our guides to skincare for hostel students and hard water skin care have simple routines.

Weekend treks

Forts and ghats near Pune are popular in the monsoon and winter. Wear sunscreen even on cloudy treks, reapply at the top, and wash your face as soon as you are back. Our hill station skin care guide has a packing list.

Pune vs Mumbai: why the same routine may not work

Many people move between the two cities. Mumbai is humid for most of the year, so lighter textures suit most skin there. Pune is drier outside the monsoon and colder in winter, so the same skin often needs more night cream from November. If you split time between them, keep one routine and change only the amount of cream. Our skin care for Mumbai weather guide covers the coastal side.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for painful or cystic acne, patches that are spreading or itchy, a rash that does not settle, or dark patches on the cheeks that keep growing. Weather-related dryness and oiliness usually settle with a simple routine; anything that hurts or keeps spreading needs a doctor.

Short answers

Is Pune weather dry or humid? Both, depending on the season. The monsoon months are humid, while November to May is mostly dry, with cool, dry winters.

Which moisturiser is best for Pune weather? A light cream that absorbs well suits the monsoon; from November, a slightly richer night cream on the cheeks and around the nose usually feels better.

Do I need sunscreen in Pune winter? Yes. Winter skies are often clear and the afternoon sun is strong, so use a broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher every morning.

Why is my skin oily in the monsoon but dry in winter? Damp, humid air makes oil and sweat sit on the skin, while cool, dry air pulls moisture out. Adjusting the amount of cream is usually enough.

Can I use night cream every night in Pune? Yes, if your skin is comfortable with it. Use a pea-sized amount in the dry months and about half in the monsoon if you are oily.

This is general skincare information, not medical advice. Patch test any new product, and see a dermatologist if you have a skin condition.
