Skin care for Mysore weather is mostly about daily sun protection and steady, light moisture: Mysuru is pleasant for much of the year, hot and dry from March to May, moderately rainy from June to November and cool and dry in the winter mornings. A gentle face wash, broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning, a light moisturiser and a thin night cream, used a little more generously from December to February, suit most skin in Mysore.

Mysuru sits on the Deccan plateau in southern Karnataka, a little lower and often a little warmer and drier than Bengaluru. Because the weather feels gentle, it is easy to skip sunscreen or moisturiser, and that is where most skin problems start.

A simple daily routine for Mysore skin

Morning

  1. Gentle face wash with lukewarm or room-temperature water.
  2. Light moisturiser: a thin layer on oily skin, more on dry cheeks in winter. Our Avocado Glow Cream 15 g (₹399) has only avocado and aloe vera and no kojic acid, so it works as a plain day or night moisturiser.
  3. Broad-spectrum sunscreen SPF 30 or higher, about two finger-lengths for face and neck, even on cool or cloudy mornings.

Night

  1. Cleanse to remove sunscreen, dust and makeup.

Mysore summer: strong sun on a "pleasant" city

March to May brings hot, bright afternoons. Mysore is usually less humid than the coast, so the main issue is sun and tan rather than stickiness.

  • Keep sunscreen on face, neck and the backs of your hands, and reapply after lunch if you are outdoors.
  • On a two-wheeler, use a helmet visor or a clean cotton face cover and full sleeves.
  • Use a light gel or lotion moisturiser rather than skipping moisture.
  • Splash your face with cool water when you come in, pat dry, then moisturise.

Dasara season: long days out and festival makeup

Mysuru Dasara, usually in September or October, means long days outdoors, crowds, evening lights and often heavy makeup for events and family functions. It also falls near the end of the rainy months, when the air can be humid.

  • Put on sunscreen before heading out for daytime processions or sightseeing.
  • Use a thin moisturiser under makeup so it sits evenly without feeling heavy.
  • Remove all makeup the same night with a gentle cleanser; do not sleep in it.
  • After late nights, keep the routine simple: cleanse, a thin night cream, sleep.

Monsoon months: light layers, no skipping sunscreen

From June to September, Mysore gets moderate rain and cooler, cloudy days. It rarely feels as sticky as Mangaluru or Mumbai, but sweat and damp clothes can still cause bumps.

  • Use lighter textures and half the usual night cream if your skin feels greasy.
  • Dry your face and skin folds fully after getting wet, and change damp clothes soon.
  • Keep sunscreen on cloudy days; daylight still reaches the skin.

Winter mornings, Coorg and Ooty trips

From December to February, Mysore mornings are cool and the air is dry. Cheeks can feel tight and lips can crack. Moisturise within a few minutes of washing, keep baths warm rather than very hot, and use a full pea-sized night cream. Our winter skin care routine covers this season in detail.

Many families drive to Coorg, Ooty or the Nilgiris on weekends. Hill air is colder and the sun at height is strong, so pack sunscreen, lip balm and a richer moisturiser. See skin care for hill station trip for a packing list.

Water at home

Many Mysore homes get piped supply water, while others use borewell or tanker water, which can be hard. If your skin feels tight or squeaky after washing, use a gentle face wash, moisturise straight away, and for sensitive skin finish with filtered or boiled-and-cooled water. Mysore vs Bengaluru and Mangaluru

Bengaluru is higher and often a little cooler, with two rainy spells; see our Bangalore weather guide. Mangaluru on the coast is humid all year with a very heavy monsoon; see the Mangalore weather guide. Mysore is closer to Bengaluru, but often a bit warmer and drier, so sunscreen and winter moisture matter most.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for sunburn with blisters, painful or cystic acne, a rash that keeps spreading, cracked skin that bleeds, or patches of colour change that keep growing. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask your doctor before using creams with active ingredients.

Short answers

What is the best skin care routine for Mysore weather? A gentle face wash, broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning, a light moisturiser and a thin night cream, a little richer from December to February.

Is Mysore weather dry or humid? Mostly mild and fairly dry, with hot dry afternoons in summer and humid spells only in the rainy months.

Do I need sunscreen in Mysore in winter? Yes. Winter days are sunny even when mornings are cool, so use SPF 30 or higher every morning.

How should I care for my skin during Mysore Dasara? Wear sunscreen for daytime events, use a thin moisturiser under makeup and remove all makeup gently the same night.

How much night cream should I use in Mysore? About half a pea-sized amount on warm, humid nights and a full pea-sized amount in the cool, dry months.

This is general skincare information, not medical advice. Patch test any new product, and see a dermatologist if you have a skin condition.
