How to choose without asking directly

  • Look at what she already uses. A gel moisturiser and blotting papers suggest oily skin; a thick cream and a lip balm in every bag suggest dry skin.
  • Notice how her skin reacts. If she often mentions redness, stinging or itching with new products, go gentle.
  • Check her routine size. Someone with one cream will enjoy one good jar. Someone with five steps may enjoy a set.
  • Ask a sister or close friend what she has been running out of.

If you still are not sure, our skin quiz takes four questions and points to a cream by skin type.

What not to give

  • Fairness creams or anything that promises to change skin colour. They send the message that her skin colour needs changing, and creams that make that promise are often the ones to be wary of. Our guide to fairness cream vs brightening cream explains the difference.
  • Strong actives for sensitive skin. If she reacts easily, skip creams with kojic acid, strong acids or retinol, and choose a plain, soothing cream.
  • Unsealed or unlabelled jars. A gift should arrive sealed, with a full ingredient list and a use-before date.
  • Anything that points at a "flaw". "For your dark spots" on a gift card rarely lands well. Keep the note about care, not correction.

Make your own skincare gift hamper

A skincare gift hamper does not need to be expensive. Start with one cream or set, then add a few small things you buy and wrap at home:

  1. A basket, box or cloth pouch you can reuse.
  2. The cream or set as the centrepiece.
  3. A soft cotton face towel and a lip balm.
  4. A sunscreen she likes, since creams with kojic acid need SPF in the morning.
  5. Something festive: a diya, a small box of dry fruits or homemade sweets.
  6. A handwritten card with two lines of use: "Patch test first. Pea-sized amount at night, sunscreen in the morning."

If you do not want the person to open a courier parcel, have it delivered to your own address and wrap it yourself.
