A simple skincare routine for dry skin in India is: a creamy, low-foam cleanser, a hydrating layer on damp skin, a richer moisturiser and sunscreen in the morning; then cleanse, hydrate and seal with a nourishing night cream before bed. The secret is not more products. It is gentler washing and layering moisture while skin is still slightly damp.

Dry skin in India gets pulled in several directions: hard water, long AC hours, hot showers and dry winter air in the north. Here is a full day and night plan, the ingredients worth looking for, and how to adjust it through the year.

Signs you have dry skin

  • Your face feels tight within 30 minutes of washing.
  • You see fine flakes around the nose, eyebrows or chin.
  • Makeup looks patchy or catches on dry spots.
  • Pores are small and you rarely look shiny, even by evening.

Not sure? Our skin quiz asks a few quick questions. Note that dry skin (low oil) is different from dehydrated skin (low water), which can happen to any skin type. Most dry skin is both, so the routine below covers each.

Morning skincare routine for dry skin in India

  1. Cleanse lightly. A cream or milky cleanser, or just lukewarm water if your skin is very dry. Avoid hot water, it strips oil fast.
  2. Hydrate on damp skin. Pat, don't rub, your face dry, then apply a hydrating toner or a hyaluronic acid serum while skin is still a little wet.
  3. Moisturise. A cream (not a gel) that feels comfortable for hours. Press it in gently.
  4. Sunscreen. SPF 30 or higher in a cream or lotion texture. Dry skin usually prefers these over matte gels.

Night routine for dry skin

  1. Cleanse. If you wore sunscreen, use a cleansing balm or oil first, then a gentle cream cleanser.
  2. Hydrate. Same toner or serum as the morning, on damp skin.
  3. Night cream. A pea-sized amount, warmed between fingers and pressed in. Add a little extra on flaky patches.
  4. Optional seal. On very dry nights, a drop of a plain face oil mixed into the cream, or a thin layer of petroleum jelly only on the driest spots.

Face cream for dry skin in winter and for flaky skin

In winter, a face cream for dry skin should feel richer than your summer one. Look for a cream that still feels soft on your cheeks 3–4 hours after applying. In the north, many people move from a lotion to a thicker cream from November to February.

A face cream for flaky skin needs both water and oil: a humectant to plump the skin and an emollient to smooth the flakes. Resist peeling or scrubbing them off. Instead:

  • Apply cream to damp skin, then a tiny extra dot on flaky areas.
  • Use a gentle exfoliant (like a mild lactic acid product) at most once a week, only if skin is not cracked or sore.
  • Run a humidifier or keep a bowl of water near the heater or AC.
  • Drink water and keep showers short and warm, not hot.

Best moisturiser for dry skin in India: how to choose

There is no single best moisturiser for dry skin in India for everyone, but the right one usually ticks these boxes:

  • Cream texture that feels comfortable for hours, not just for a few minutes.
  • Both humectants and emollients in the ingredient list.
  • Little or no fragrance or drying alcohol near the top of the list.
  • A size you can finish in 2–3 months, so it stays fresh.

Best night cream for dry skin in India: what to look for

At night your cream can be richer than in the day, because it doesn't need to sit under sunscreen. Look for plant oils, aloe vera or glycerin, and avoid stacking several strong actives. If your night cream has kojic acid, use sunscreen every morning.

Habits that make dry skin worse

  • Hot showers and washing your face with bathing soap.
  • Foaming face washes twice a day.
  • Waiting too long after washing to apply cream.
  • Daily scrubs or peel-off masks.
  • Skipping sunscreen in winter, when UV is still strong in India.

See a dermatologist if you have red, itchy or cracked patches that don't settle, possible eczema, or dryness along with other symptoms. Ask before starting new actives if you are pregnant.

Short answers

What is the simplest routine for dry skin? Gentle cleanser, cream moisturiser on damp skin and sunscreen in the morning; cleanser and a nourishing night cream at night.

Should dry skin use face wash daily? A mild cream cleanser once or twice a day is fine. In very dry weather, rinse with water in the morning.

Which ingredients help a dry skin moisturiser? A mix of humectants (glycerin, hyaluronic acid, aloe vera) and emollients (plant oils, butters).

Is a gel moisturiser enough for dry skin? Usually not on its own. Layer it under a cream, or switch to a cream.

How quickly will dry skin feel better? Most people feel more comfortable within days, and the look of the skin settles over a few weeks.

This is general skincare information, not medical advice. Patch test any new product, and see a dermatologist if you have a skin condition.
