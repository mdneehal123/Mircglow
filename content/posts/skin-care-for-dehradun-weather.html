Skin care for Dehradun weather means planning for four quite different seasons: a hot, fairly dry summer from April to June, one of the wettest monsoons of any north Indian city from July to September, a short pleasant autumn and a cold, damp-to-dry winter from December to February. A gentle face wash and broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning stay the same all year, while your moisturiser and night cream go light in the monsoon and richer in winter.

Dehradun sits in the Doon valley between the Shivalik hills and the lower Himalaya, so it is cooler than the plains in winter, much rainier in the monsoon and only a short drive from colder hill towns like Mussoorie.

A simple daily routine for Dehradun skin

Morning

  1. Gentle face wash with lukewarm water. On cold mornings, resist very hot water; it strips the skin.
  2. Moisturiser: a thin layer in the monsoon, a little more on cheeks in winter. Our Avocado Glow Cream 15 g (₹399) has only avocado and aloe vera and no kojic acid, so it works as a plain day moisturiser.
  3. Broad-spectrum sunscreen SPF 30 or higher, about two finger-lengths for face and neck. Clear winter days in the valley still have strong sun.

Night

  1. Cleanse to remove sunscreen, sweat and dust.
  2. Night cream: about half a pea-sized amount on humid monsoon nights, a full pea-sized amount from December to February. Our Avocado Night Cream (30 g, ₹649) has avocado, aloe vera, vitamin B3 and kojic acid; if you use it, wear sunscreen every morning.
  3. Lip balm before sleep in winter.

Dehradun summer: sun, dust and dry heat

April to June can be properly hot in the valley, with dusty roads and strong afternoon sun. Skin often tans quickly here because people expect a hill city to be cooler than it is.

  • Reapply sunscreen after lunch if you are outdoors, and cover your face on a scooter.
  • Use a light lotion or gel-cream moisturiser rather than skipping moisturiser.
  • Splash your face with cool water when you come in and drink water through the day.
  • A gentle evening cleanse removes dust; you do not need a scrub.

If your face feels hot and tight after a day out, our after-sun skincare guide helps that evening, and skincare for bike riders covers daily rides. Tan fades slowly over weeks with shade and daily sunscreen; no cream removes it overnight.

Monsoon in the Doon valley

July to September is the season Dehradun is known for: long spells of heavy rain, damp air and clothes that never quite dry. For skin, the main problems are trapped sweat, greasiness and fungal-type itching in folds.

  • Switch to lighter textures and about half your usual night cream if your face feels greasy.
  • Dry your face, neck, underarms and toes fully after getting wet.
  • Change damp clothes and socks as soon as you reach home.
  • Wash pillow covers and face towels often, and dry towels in the sun when you can.

Winter in Dehradun: cold mornings, heaters and dry cheeks

From December to February, mornings are foggy and cold, nights are chilly and most homes use heaters or blowers. Add hot baths and the skin barrier struggles.

  1. Keep baths warm, not very hot, and short.
  2. Moisturise within three minutes of washing, while skin is still a little damp.
  3. Use a full pea-sized night cream on cheeks and around the nose, and lip balm before sleep.
  4. If you sleep with a heater on, keep a bowl of water in the room and moisturise before bed; see our room heater skin care guide.
  5. Keep sunscreen in the routine; winter sun is easy to underestimate.

Weekend trips to Mussoorie and higher

Many Dehradun families drive up to Mussoorie, Dhanaulti or Chakrata on weekends. The air up there is colder, windier and drier, and the sun at height is stronger than it feels. Pack a richer moisturiser, lip balm and sunscreen, and reapply on long walks. Our hill station trip checklist and long car drive guide cover the rest.

Students and hostels in Dehradun

Dehradun has many schools, colleges and coaching centres, so a lot of young people live in hostels and PGs here. Keep a short kit: face wash, one moisturiser, sunscreen and lip balm. Do not share towels, and dry damp clothes properly in the monsoon. Our skincare for hostel students guide has a budget list.

Dehradun vs Chandigarh and Delhi

Chandigarh, also at the foot of the hills, has a drier monsoon and similar foggy winters; see our Chandigarh weather guide. Delhi has a hotter summer and much heavier smog in winter; see the Delhi weather guide. Dehradun's big difference is its long, wet monsoon, so the monsoon changes above matter most here.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for sunburn with blisters, painful or cystic acne, an itchy rash in skin folds that keeps spreading in the monsoon, cracked skin that bleeds in winter, or patches of colour change that keep growing. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask your doctor before using creams with active ingredients.

Short answers

What is the best skin care routine for Dehradun weather? A gentle face wash, a moisturiser, broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning and a night cream. Go lighter in the rainy months and richer from December to February.

Why does my skin get oily in the Dehradun monsoon? The air stays very humid for weeks, so sweat and oil do not dry off. Use lighter layers, half the night cream and dry your skin fully after getting wet.

How do I stop dry skin in Dehradun winter? Warm (not hot) baths, moisturiser on damp skin, a full pea-sized night cream and lip balm at night, plus a bowl of water near the heater.

Do I need sunscreen in Dehradun in winter? Yes. Clear winter days in the valley and in the hills above still tan the skin, so keep sunscreen every morning.

Is Dehradun weather like Mussoorie? No. Mussoorie is higher, colder and windier, so it needs a richer cream and more lip care than the valley.
