Skin care for Kolkata weather means a light, humid-weather routine for most of the year: a gentle face wash, a thin moisturiser, a broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning and a proper cleanse at night. Kolkata is hot and sticky from about March to October, with a long monsoon in the middle, so heavy creams usually feel greasy; only the short winter from about December to February calls for a little more moisture.

The city adds its own twists: sudden pre-monsoon storms, long Durga Puja nights in the heat, smoky, hazy winter evenings, crowded buses and metro rides, and cold AC offices. Here is one simple routine and how to adjust it through the Kolkata year.

Sunscreen in Kolkata, even on cloudy days

Kolkata sun is strong from mid-morning for most of the year, and grey monsoon or hazy winter skies still let a lot of UV through.

  • Use a broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher as the last morning step, about two finger-lengths for face and neck.
  • Gel or fluid sunscreens usually feel less heavy in humidity than thick creams.
  • Reapply before going out again after lunch, and after wiping a sweaty face.
  • Ferry rides, rooftop afternoons and long walks by the river count as sun time; a cap or umbrella helps.

A simple daily routine for Kolkata skin

Morning

  1. Gentle face wash with cool or lukewarm water. No scrubbing.

Night

Monsoon in Kolkata: June to September

The rainy season is long, and streets can stay waterlogged after a heavy spell. Damp skin and damp clothes together cause most monsoon trouble.

  • Carry a small towel or tissues. Pat your face dry after getting wet, then add a little moisturiser only if skin feels tight.
  • Change out of wet clothes and socks as soon as you can, and keep the neck, underarms and feet dry.
  • If you walked through flood water, wash your feet and legs with soap and clean water soon after, and see a doctor for any cut or wound.
  • Pause new actives and scrubs if your skin feels itchy or irritated.

Durga Puja and festival nights

Pandal hopping often means hours outdoors in warm, humid weather, heavy makeup, crowds and very late nights, for several days in a row. Skin does not need anything new for this; it needs the basics done well.

  • Do not start a new cream or a facial in the week before Puja. Try anything new at least two weeks earlier.
  • Wear sunscreen under makeup for daytime visits, and keep tissues for blotting.
  • However late you get home, remove makeup and wash your face before sleeping.
  • Drink water through the day and sleep in when you can. Our guide on skin care for lack of sleep has a short routine for the morning after.

Kolkata winter: drier air and hazy evenings

Winter is short, but the air is drier, mornings are cool and many evenings are hazy with smoke and dust. Skin that was oily all year can suddenly feel tight on the cheeks.

  • Use a little more night cream on the cheeks and around the nose, but keep the T-zone light if it still gets oily.
  • Bathe with lukewarm water. Very hot baths feel good in the cold but leave skin drier.
  • On smoky days, cleanse gently at night instead of scrubbing; our skincare for air pollution guide explains the routine.
  • Moisturise hands after washing and keep a lip balm in your bag.

Buses, metro and AC offices

A normal day can mean a sticky bus or auto ride, a crowded metro, then hours under cold AC. That mix leaves the T-zone shiny and the cheeks tight at the same time. Short answers

Which moisturiser is best for Kolkata weather? A light gel or thin cream that absorbs quickly suits Kolkata's humid months; switch to a slightly richer cream only in the short winter if your cheeks feel tight.

Do I need sunscreen in Kolkata in the monsoon and winter? Yes. Clouds and haze still let much of the UV through, so apply a broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher every morning.

Why does my skin get dry in Kolkata winter when it is oily all year? The winter air is cooler and drier, and hot baths add to it. Oily skin can still feel tight, so add a little cream only where it feels dry.

How should I care for skin during Durga Puja? Stick to your usual routine, wear sunscreen for daytime visits, blot instead of washing again and again, and always remove makeup before bed.

Can I use night cream in Kolkata's humidity? Yes, in a thin layer on clean skin. Oily skin can use half a pea-sized amount and keep the nose and forehead light.

This is general skincare information, not medical advice. Patch test any new product, and see a dermatologist if you have a skin condition.
