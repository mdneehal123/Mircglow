The best Diwali skincare routine is a simple one you start now and keep steady: gentle cleansing, a good moisturiser, sunscreen every morning and a night cream every night, with no new products in the last two weeks. With Diwali on Sunday 8 November 2026, you have about 19 days. That is enough for skin that looks hydrated, smooth and rested in photos, but not enough to change dark spots or tone, which usually take 8–12 weeks.

Below is a day-by-day countdown, a festival-day routine around puja, make-up and diyas, and a plan for the days after, when crackers, smoke and late nights catch up with your skin.

What 19 days can and cannot do

So the goal for this Diwali is a fresh, comfortable glow. If you want help with spots or tone, start now anyway: the same routine carries you into wedding season, which runs roughly November to February.

The countdown: 20 October to 8 November

Now to 25 October: set the base

  • Fix your basics. A mild face wash, a moisturiser, SPF 30 or more every morning and a night cream at night. If you are unsure where to start, our simple night skincare routine takes four steps.
  • Patch test anything new this week. Apply a little behind the ear or on the jawline for 2–3 nights. After about 25 October, stop adding new products.
  • Book the parlour early. If you usually get a facial, book it for 29 October to 1 November, so it lands 7–10 days before Diwali and any redness settles. Our guide to night cream after a facial covers the aftercare.

26 October to 4 November: Diwali safai and shopping

  • Cleaning the house? Dust, old paint and cleaning liquids are hard on skin. Wear gloves and a cotton dupatta or mask over the nose and mouth, and wash your face and hands when you finish.
  • Kitchen heat. Frying namkeen and making sweets means steam and oil near your face. Rinse with plain water afterwards rather than scrubbing.
  • Exfoliate gently once a week, no more. Skip it if your skin is sensitive or red.
  • Sunscreen for market days. Hours of outdoor shopping, including on Dhanteras (Friday 6 November), mean sun exposure even in the cooler weather.

5–7 November: final prep

  • Threading or waxing by 5 or 6 November. Skip kojic acid, glutathione and other actives on that skin for 24–48 hours and use a plain, soothing cream instead. See night cream after waxing and threading.
  • The night before Diwali: cleanse, apply a hydrating serum or toner on damp skin, then a slightly generous layer of night cream and plenty of lip balm. A rich cream like Avocado Night Cream helps skin feel soft and moisturised by morning.
  • Sleep. It is the most underrated glow step of the whole season.

Your Diwali skincare routine on the festival day

Morning

  1. Rinse with lukewarm water, not hot. Many Indian homes have hard water, which can leave skin feeling tight, so moisturise straight after.
  2. Apply a light moisturiser on slightly damp skin.
  3. Sunscreen, SPF 30 or more, especially if you use kojic acid or vitamin C at night.
  4. Lip balm.

Before puja and make-up

  1. If your skin feels oily by evening, rinse with plain water and pat dry.
  2. A thin layer of a light cream gives make-up a soft base. Avocado Glow Cream 15 g has avocado and aloe vera only, so it sits well under make-up and suits sensitive skin.
  3. Wait five minutes before primer or foundation.
  4. For extra glow, a touch of highlighter on the cheekbones is the honest instant option.

Around diyas and crackers

  • Keep loose dupattas, hair and sprays away from flames. Hairspray and some setting sprays are flammable.
  • Wash your hands after handling sparklers or crackers before touching your face.
  • If you stand in thick smoke for long, step away for fresh air and rinse your face when you get the chance.

The night of Diwali: take it all off

However late it is, do not sleep in make-up and smoke. This is the step that decides how your skin looks for Bhai Dooj on Wednesday 11 November.

  1. First cleanse: an oil cleanser or balm to melt make-up and sunscreen.
  2. Second cleanse: a gentle face wash to clear soot and residue. Wipes alone are not enough.
  3. Night cream: a full pea-sized amount, pressed in gently. Our guide on how to apply night cream shows the technique.
  4. Lip balm and a clean pillowcase.

Skincare after Diwali: crackers, smoke and late nights

After Diwali, many north Indian cities see heavy smoke and smog for days, and skin often feels dry, dull or a little irritated. Plan a calm recovery week from 9 to 15 November.

Dull, tired-looking skinDouble cleanse at night, night cream, extra sleep
Dryness and tightnessMoisturise on damp skin morning and night; shorter, cooler showers
Small breakoutsKeep the routine gentle; do not squeeze; a light gel moisturiser by day
Stinging or rednessPause actives for a few days and use only a plain, soothing cream
Dry, chapped lipsLip balm several times a day and overnight
  • Skip scrubs and peels for the first few days. Irritated skin needs comfort, not more friction.
  • Keep sunscreen going even on hazy days.
  • Wash your face when you come in from outdoors during the smoggy days.

Once your skin feels comfortable again, you can bring back your usual actives. Our winter skincare routine for India is the natural next step as the air gets drier.

Adjust for your skin type

  • Oily skin: a light gel moisturiser by day and a thin layer of night cream. Blotting papers on the night beat layers of powder. See night cream for oily skin.
  • Dry skin: moisturise within a minute of washing, and use a hydrating primer under make-up.
  • Sensitive skin: no exfoliation in the last week, no new products, and only the products you know your skin likes.

If you have painful or cystic acne, a rash, melasma, or you are pregnant, see a dermatologist before using active ingredients. Short answers

When should I start a Diwali skincare routine? Now. Nineteen days of steady basics give skin that looks hydrated and rested; change in tone needs 8–12 weeks.

When should I get a facial before Diwali? Between 29 October and 1 November, so it is 7–10 days before the festival.

Can I try a new cream the week of Diwali? It is risky. Patch test new products by about 25 October, then keep your routine the same.

How do I clean my face after Diwali crackers? Double cleanse: an oil cleanser for make-up and sunscreen, then a gentle face wash, followed by a night cream.

Why does my skin look dull after Diwali? Late nights, smoke and make-up add up. Give it a gentle week of cleansing, moisturising and sleep.

This is general skincare information, not medical advice. Patch test any new product, and see a dermatologist if you have a skin condition.
