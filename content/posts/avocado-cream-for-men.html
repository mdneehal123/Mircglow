Do men really need a face cream?

Most men wash their face with whatever soap is in the bathroom and stop there. Soap, hard water, sun, helmet sweat and shaving all leave skin feeling tight, rough or dull.

A moisturiser helps skin feel comfortable and look healthier. It is not about looking "made up". It is like oiling a bike chain: a small habit that keeps things running smoothly. Our night cream for men guide covers the basics if you are completely new.

How to use avocado cream for men: a 1-minute routine

Night (main step)

  1. Wash your face with a mild face wash, not body soap.
  2. Pat dry with a clean towel.
  3. Take a pea-sized amount and dot it on forehead, cheeks, nose and chin.
  4. Spread it in thinly. Use a little less on the nose and forehead if they get oily.
  5. Do not forget the neck, especially if you shave there.

Morning

  1. Splash with water or use a mild face wash.
  2. If skin feels dry, use a rice-grain amount of cream.
  3. Apply sunscreen SPF 30 or higher. Every day. This matters most.

Using it around shaving

Shaving scrapes off a thin layer of skin along with the hair, which can leave the beard area dry, red or bumpy.

  • Shave at night if you can. Then the cream has all night to settle on fresh skin.
  • Wait 5 to 10 minutes after shaving before applying cream, so any small nicks can settle.
  • Skip broken skin. Do not apply on cuts, razor burn that stings, or open bumps.
  • Use a fresh, sharp blade and shave with the grain to reduce irritation.
  • Do a patch test first on freshly shaved skin, since it can be more reactive.

If you get razor bumps often, try shaving less closely, or every second day, and give your skin a break on weekends. Gentle, regular moisturising helps the beard area feel smoother between shaves.

If you have a beard, apply the cream to the skin under it with your fingertips, focusing on cheeks and any dry patches.

Common mistakes men make

  • Using body soap or detergent-heavy face washes daily.
  • Applying a big scoop of cream. A pea is enough.
  • Skipping sunscreen. Bike rides and outdoor work mean a lot of sun.
  • Using aftershave with strong alcohol right before cream.
  • Giving up after a week. Skin renews roughly every 4 to 6 weeks, so a more even look usually takes 8 to 12 weeks.
  • Keeping the jar in a hot car or bike seat compartment. Store it somewhere cool and close the lid tightly.
