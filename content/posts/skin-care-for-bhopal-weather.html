Skin care for Bhopal weather changes a lot with the seasons: the city has a very hot, dry summer from April to June, a humid monsoon from July to September and a cool, dry winter from November to February. A gentle face wash and broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning stay the same all year, while your moisturiser and night cream go light in the monsoon and richer in the dry summer and winter months.

Bhopal sits on the Malwa plateau in central India, far from the sea, so the air is dry for much of the year. That makes dehydration, tan and tight cheeks more common than constant stickiness, except in the rainy months.

A simple daily routine for Bhopal skin

Morning

  1. Gentle face wash with lukewarm water. Very hot water dries the skin further, especially in winter.
  2. Moisturiser: a thin layer in the monsoon, a little more on dry cheeks in summer and winter. Our Avocado Glow Cream 15 g (₹399) has only avocado and aloe vera and no kojic acid, so it works as a plain day or night moisturiser.
  3. Broad-spectrum sunscreen SPF 30 or higher, about two finger-lengths for face and neck, on sunny and cloudy days.

Night

  1. Cleanse to remove sunscreen, sweat and dust.
  2. Night cream: about half a pea-sized amount on humid monsoon nights, a full pea-sized amount from November to February and on dry summer nights. Our Avocado Night Cream (30 g, ₹649) has avocado, aloe vera, vitamin B3 and kojic acid; if you use it, wear sunscreen every morning.
  3. Lip balm before sleep in winter and in the hot-wind weeks.

Bhopal summer: dry heat and hot winds

April to June is the hardest stretch. The heat is intense, the air is dry and hot winds in late summer pull water from the skin. Many people find their face feels oily on the forehead and nose but tight on the cheeks at the same time.

  • Stay in shade in the middle of the day where you can, and cover up with a cotton dupatta, cap or full sleeves.
  • Reapply sunscreen after lunch if you are outdoors, and cover your face on a two-wheeler.
  • Use a light lotion moisturiser; do not skip it just because it is hot.
  • Drink water through the day and splash your face with cool (not ice-cold) water when you come in.
  • Coolers add a little moisture to the room; AC dries it, so moisturise more if you sit in AC all day.

Monsoon in the City of Lakes

From July to September, Bhopal turns green and humid. Sweat does not dry easily, and clothes can stay damp after a sudden shower.

  • Switch to lighter textures and half the usual night cream if your skin feels greasy.
  • Dry your face, neck and skin folds fully after getting wet.
  • Change damp clothes, socks and footwear soon after reaching home.
  • Wash pillow covers and face towels often.

Winter in Bhopal: cold nights and dry air

From November to February, days are pleasant but mornings and nights get cold, and the air is dry. This is when cheeks, lips and hands feel rough.

  1. Keep baths warm, not very hot, and keep them short.
  2. Moisturise within three minutes of washing, while skin is still a little damp.
  3. Use a full pea-sized night cream on cheeks and around the nose, and lip balm before sleep.
  4. If you use a room heater, keep a bowl of water in the room and moisturise before bed; see our room heater skin care guide.
  5. Do not skip sunscreen in winter; the sun on a clear winter day still tans.

Hard water and dust at home

Many Bhopal homes use borewell water, which can be hard and leave skin feeling squeaky after washing. Use a gentle face wash, moisturise straight after and, if your skin is sensitive, finish with a splash of filtered water. Our hard water skin care guide explains more. Dusty roads and construction can also leave a film on the skin; a gentle evening cleanse is enough, without scrubbing.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for sunburn with blisters, painful or cystic acne, cracked skin that bleeds, an itchy rash in skin folds that keeps spreading, or patches of colour change that keep growing. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask your doctor before using creams with active ingredients.

Short answers

What is the best skin care routine for Bhopal weather? A gentle face wash, a moisturiser, broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning and a night cream. Use lighter layers in the monsoon and richer ones in the dry summer and winter.

Why does my skin feel so dry in Bhopal winter? Winter air in central India is cool and dry, and hot baths and heaters add to it. Moisturise on damp skin and use a full pea-sized night cream.

How do I protect my skin in Bhopal summer? Shade, cover-ups and sunscreen reapplied after lunch, plus a light moisturiser so dry heat does not leave skin tight.

How much night cream should I use in Bhopal? About half a pea-sized amount on humid monsoon nights and a full pea-sized amount in summer and winter.

Is Bhopal weather like Indore? Quite similar: both have dry summers, a humid monsoon and cool dry winters, so the same routine works with small changes.
