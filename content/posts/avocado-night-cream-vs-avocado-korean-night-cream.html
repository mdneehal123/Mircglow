What is different: texture

This is the real choice. The Avocado Night Cream is richer and more cushioned. It leaves a comforting layer that dry skin drinks up overnight, which is why it suits winter in Delhi or Jaipur, or anyone whose face feels tight after a day in AC.

The Avocado Korean Night Cream is smooth and medium-weight, made in a Korean-inspired style. It spreads easily, sinks in without a heavy film, and sits comfortably under a fan on a humid night in Mumbai, Chennai or Kochi. It suits normal, combination and oily skin, and dry skin in humid months.

Neither is "stronger". They simply feel different on the skin.

Can you use both?

Yes, but not on the same night. Both contain kojic acid, so layering them doubles the kojic acid without adding anything new, and raises the chance of irritation. Better ways to own both:

  • Switch with the seasons. The Korean cream from roughly March to October, the richer cream in the cooler, drier months.
  • Switch with your setting. The richer one on nights after a long AC day, the Korean one on hot, humid nights.
  • Share between two people in a home with different skin types.

If your skin is sensitive and you want a plain moisturiser on some nights, our Avocado Glow Cream 15 g has avocado and aloe vera only, with no kojic acid.

How to use either one

  1. Patch test behind your ear for 24–48 hours.
  2. At night, cleanse and pat your face dry.
  3. Warm a pea-sized amount between your fingertips and press it over the face and neck.
  4. Use every other night for the first two weeks, then nightly.
  5. Wear SPF 30 or higher every morning.
