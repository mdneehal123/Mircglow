Small gifting etiquette that matters

  • Keep the jar sealed. Don't open it to "check". A sealed jar looks better, feels more hygienic, and our 7-day returns only apply to sealed jars.
  • Add a note card. Something short like "Try a little behind your ear or on the side of your neck for a day before using it on your face." It's caring, not preachy.
  • Mention sunscreen. Most of our avocado creams and the Glutathione Cream contain kojic acid, so a line like "sunscreen in the morning" on the card is genuinely helpful.
  • Don't promise a change in skin colour. It won't, and it isn't a kind thing to imply. These creams are about soft, comfortable, fresh-looking skin.
  • Pair it simply. A set with a diya, a box of dry fruits or a handwritten card says plenty.
