What avocado cream is really for

Avocado brings natural oils and a soft, buttery feel. In a night cream, that means a layer that sits on your skin while you sleep and keeps it from drying out. If your face feels tight ten minutes after washing, or you get flaky patches beside the nose in a Delhi winter, this is the cream that makes the most difference, fast.

What glutathione cream is really for

Glutathione is an antioxidant that shows up in brightening-focused skincare. In a cream, think of it as one part of a team aimed at a fresher, more radiant look. Our Glutathione Cream pairs it with vitamin C, kojic acid, niacinamide and aloe vera.

Let's be clear about one thing, because the internet isn't. Glutathione cream does not change your natural skin colour. It isn't meant to change skin tone, and anyone selling it that way is overselling. What it can do, with steady use and daily sunscreen, is help skin look brighter, less dull and more even over a few months. The glutathione cream benefits guide goes into the detail.

Texture and how each feels in Indian weather

This matters more than people expect. A cream you dislike the feel of is a cream you'll stop using by week two.

  • Chennai or Mumbai humidity: both work, in a thin layer. With either one, half a pea is often plenty, and let it settle under the fan before bed.
  • Delhi or Lucknow winter, heater on: avocado wins. Dry air pulls water from skin all night, and a richer cream holds it in.
  • AC office, AC bedroom: avocado at night for comfort. Even oily skin can feel tight after a full day under the vent.
  • Oily or pimple-prone skin: start with a very thin layer every second night, or try the gentle Avocado Glow Cream 15g (₹399) first. See avocado cream for oily skin.

Who each one suits (and who should skip)

Pick avocado cream if

Your skin feels dry, tight or rough, you're in your 30s and noticing skin isn't as plump-feeling as before, or you just want one simple night cream at a fair price. It's also the easier start if you've never used a night cream.

Pick glutathione cream if

Your skin is fairly comfortable already but looks flat, tired or uneven, especially after a summer of two-wheeler rides and sun. You're happy to wear sunscreen every morning and wait a couple of months.

Honestly, skip glutathione cream if

You're hoping it will change your skin colour. It won't, and you'll be disappointed. Also skip it for now if your skin is irritated, peeling or freshly sunburnt. Calm it first with a gentle moisturiser. If you have melasma or patches that are spreading, see a dermatologist before buying anything, including ours.

Can you use avocado cream and glutathione cream together?

Yes, and this is how a lot of people get the best of both. You don't need to layer them on top of each other the same night. Two easy ways:

  1. Alternate nights. Glutathione cream one night, avocado cream the next. Simple, and each jar lasts twice as long.
  2. Glutathione most nights, avocado when skin feels dry. Good for winter or after a long day in AC.
  3. Light morning, rich night. If you prefer, a thin layer of glutathione cream in the morning under sunscreen, and avocado cream at night. Sunscreen is not optional here.

Both of our creams contain kojic acid, so don't double up on extra brightening serums at the start. Add one new product at a time, two weeks apart, and patch test each one on the side of your neck or behind the ear for a day or two first.
