After a face pack, rinse it off with lukewarm water, pat your face dry, and apply a moisturiser or night cream within a minute while the skin is still slightly damp. This matters most after clay packs like multani mitti, which soak up oil and can leave skin tight; a plain, soothing cream is the safest choice that night.

Home packs with multani mitti, besan, curd, haldi or rose water are part of many Indian routines. Here is what to put on afterwards, what to skip, and how to time a face pack with your night cream, sunscreen and active creams.

What to apply after a face pack: the 5-step order

  1. Do not let it crack. Rinse clay packs while they are still a little damp, usually 10 to 15 minutes after applying. A fully dried, cracking pack pulls water out of the skin.
  2. Rinse with lukewarm water using your fingertips. Do not rub the pack off with a towel; that turns it into a scrub.
  3. Skip the face wash. If you rinse well, a second wash is not needed and only dries the skin further.
  4. Optional: a splash of rose water or plain water to remove the last traces of pack.
  5. Moisturise within a minute. A pea-sized amount of cream for the face and a little more for the neck. See how to moisturise skin overnight for the damp-skin method.

What to apply after multani mitti

Multani mitti (fuller's earth) is a clay. It absorbs oil, which is why oily skin feels fresh after it, but it can leave normal and dry skin feeling tight. So after multani mitti:

  • Oily skin: a light gel or a thin layer of cream. Do not skip moisturiser just because your face feels clean; tight skin often makes more oil later.
  • Normal or combination skin: a regular night cream, a little more on the cheeks than on the T-zone.
  • Dry skin: a richer cream, applied quickly. Many people with dry skin are better off mixing multani mitti with curd or aloe gel instead of plain water, or choosing a non-clay pack.

Can I apply night cream after a face pack?

Yes. Night cream after a face pack is a good habit, because the pack has taken off oil and the top layer of dead skin and the face is ready to hold moisture. The only question is which night cream:

  • If your cream has kojic acid, glutathione, vitamin C or any other active, and your skin feels warm, tight or tingly after the pack, use a plain cream that night and go back to your active cream the next night.
  • If your skin feels completely normal, your usual night cream is fine. Patch test the pack itself first if it is new to you; see how to patch test a face cream.

Face pack in the morning or at night?

Night is easier. You can follow the pack with night cream and let the skin settle while you sleep. If you use a pack in the morning, follow it with a light moisturiser and then sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, before going out. Never go into the sun straight after a clay or haldi pack without sunscreen.

How often to use a face pack

  • Oily skin: a clay pack once or twice a week.
  • Normal or combination skin: once a week, or clay only on the T-zone.
  • Dry or sensitive skin: once a week at most, with soothing ingredients like curd, honey or aloe rather than plain clay.

Using a pack daily, or mixing in lemon, baking soda or toothpaste, often leaves skin red and dry. If you want brighter-looking skin, a steady routine does more; see night cream for dull skin.

Mistakes after a face pack

  • Leaving the pack on until it cracks and flakes.
  • Scrubbing it off or using a scrub the same night.
  • Washing again with a strong face wash or soap.
  • Skipping moisturiser because skin "feels clean".
  • Applying a new active cream for the first time on the same night as a pack.
  • Going into the sun without sunscreen.

If a pack ever leaves you with burning, swelling, a rash or itchy bumps, rinse it off, use nothing but a plain moisturiser, and see a dermatologist if it does not settle.

Short answers

What should I apply after a multani mitti face pack? A moisturiser or night cream on slightly damp skin, within a minute of rinsing. Clay can leave skin tight, so do not skip it.

Should I wash my face with face wash after a face pack? Usually no. A good lukewarm rinse is enough; a second wash can dry the skin.

Can I apply rose water after a face pack? Yes, as a final splash, but follow it with a cream. Rose water alone does not keep skin moisturised.

Can I use kojic acid cream after a face pack? If your skin feels normal, yes. If it feels tight, warm or tingly, use a plain cream that night instead.

How long should I keep a multani mitti pack on? About 10 to 15 minutes, and rinse it while it is still slightly damp.
