A higher price does not always mean more glutathione or better results. Some of it can go on packaging, advertising or import costs. Equally, a very cheap jar making big promises deserves a second look.

How to work out price per gram

Divide the price by the grams in the jar. For example, ₹1,499 ÷ 30 g is about ₹50 per gram. Now you can compare a 15 g jar and a 50 g jar fairly.

Then think per month. A pea-sized amount for the face plus a little for the neck is roughly 0.3–0.5 g a night, so most people use about 10–15 g a month. At ₹50 per gram, that is about ₹500–₹750 a month.

Also check whether the size is in grams or millilitres. Creams are usually sold by weight, and ml is close but not the same.

What makes a glutathione cream cost more

  • Supporting actives. Vitamin C, niacinamide, alpha arbutin or hyaluronic acid all add cost.
  • Stability. Glutathione and vitamin C can break down with air and light, so better jars and airtight packaging cost more.
  • Jar size. Small jars often look cheap but cost more per gram.
  • Brand and import costs. These raise the price without changing what you put on your face.
