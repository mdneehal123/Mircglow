There is no single best night cream for glowing skin. There is the one that is best for you. It suits your skin type, it has ingredients that match what you want, and it feels nice enough that you still reach for it on the nights you are tired.

That last part matters more than people admit. An expensive cream you forget to use loses to a modest one you use every night. So this guide is a checklist you can apply to any brand, ours included.

Why a night cream helps with glow

Glow is mostly two things: hydration and an even surface. At night your skin is clean, with no sunscreen, no make-up and no sun on it. A cream can sit there for seven or eight hours. Skin that stays moisturised through the night reflects light better in the morning, and that reflection is what we call glow.

7 things to check before you buy

  1. Do the ingredients match your goal? The list is just below.
  2. Is the texture right for your skin? Rich for dry skin, lighter for oily.
  3. Is there a full ingredient list? If a product hides what is in it, put it back.
  4. Is the manufacturer named? Look for a batch number and a use-before date as well.
  5. What is the price per gram? Jars come in every size. Divide the price by the weight before comparing.
  6. Are the claims believable? A cosmetic can improve how skin looks. It cannot transform it in days.
  7. Will you finish it? A small jar used nightly beats a big one that sits around for a year.

Ingredients worth looking for

  • Niacinamide (vitamin B3). Smooth, even-looking tone. Gentle.
  • Vitamin C. The classic antioxidant for radiance.
  • Kojic acid. For the look of dark spots and tan. Needs sunscreen by day.
  • Glutathione. For an overall fresh, bright look.
  • Hyaluronic acid. Hydration and a plump look.
  • Avocado, shea or squalane. Nourishment and softness.
  • Retinol. Popular for a smoother look, but it needs a careful start and does not suit everyone.

Any of these can be looked up in our skincare ingredient glossary.

Choosing by skin type

Dry. Go for a rich cream with something nourishing like avocado, and use a full pea-sized amount.

Oily. Choose a lighter texture, or use a rich cream very thinly. Skipping moisturiser altogether tends to make skin look shinier. See night cream for oily skin.

Combination. One cream, but more on the cheeks and less on the forehead and nose.

Sensitive. Simple formulas, a patch test, and a slow start with anything active such as kojic acid.

Choosing by age

  • Twenties. Hydration and sunscreen. A simple moisturising cream is enough.
  • Thirties. Add antioxidants such as vitamin C or glutathione.
  • Forty and over. Richer textures, used every night without fail.

How to use it for the most glow

  1. Cleanse. Twice, if you wore sunscreen or make-up.
  2. Toner or serum, if you use them.
  3. A pea-sized amount of night cream, pressed into the face and neck.
  4. Sunscreen in the morning.

The technique is in how to apply night cream.

How soon will you see it?

Softer skin after the first night. More hydrated within a week. A more even-looking tone in six to twelve weeks of nightly use.

What dulls your glow

  • Sleeping in sunscreen or make-up.
  • Skipping sunscreen in the morning.
  • Too much cream.
  • A new product every fortnight.

Short answers

Which night cream is best for glowing skin? One that suits your skin type and has ingredients such as niacinamide, vitamin C, kojic acid or glutathione.

Can I use it in the day? Night creams are usually too rich for an Indian morning. Use a lighter cream and sunscreen.

Can men use it? Yes.

Do I really need one? If you want softer, more hydrated-looking skin when you wake up, it is the most useful single thing you can do in the evening.
