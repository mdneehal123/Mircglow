Yes. You can smooth your avocado night cream down the neck after applying it on your face. Use a thin layer, patch test first, because neck skin is thin and sensitive, and put sunscreen on your neck every morning. A cream can help the skin feel softer and look more even, but a dark neck can have other causes, so see a doctor if it is sudden or velvety.

How to apply it on the neck

  1. Patch test on the side of the neck and wait 24 hours.
  2. Apply your face cream first.
  3. Use what is left on your fingers, plus a tiny extra amount, on the neck.
  4. Smooth upward from the collarbone to the jaw.
  5. Skip the front of the throat if it feels irritated.

Why the neck needs sun protection too

The neck gets sun every day but is often forgotten. Without sunscreen, tan and darkness return. Apply sunscreen to your neck and the back of the neck every morning.

Common reasons for a dark neck

  • Sun exposure and tan.
  • Friction from collars, chains or rubbing.
  • Dry, rough skin that traps dead cells.
  • Medical reasons, such as insulin resistance, that need a doctor to check.

If the darkness is thick, velvety or has appeared quickly, see a doctor before using any cream.

What to expect

Softer skin comes quickly. A more even look takes 8 to 12 weeks of regular use with sunscreen. Results vary.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

Can I use face cream on my neck?

Yes, most people can. Patch test first and use a thin layer.

Will avocado night cream lighten a dark neck?

It may help the skin look smoother and more even over weeks. It does not treat the medical causes of darkness.

How often should I apply it?

Once a night, after the face. Start every other night.

Do I need sunscreen on my neck?

Yes, every morning, including the back of the neck.
