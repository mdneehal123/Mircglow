A centella cream, also called a cica cream, is a moisturiser made with centella asiatica, a plant long loved in Korean skincare for its calm, comfortable feel. For Indian skin it is a good everyday choice when heat, sweat, sun or AC leave your face feeling stressed, tight or easily red.

It is not a strong active. Think of it as the soothing-feeling, supportive part of a routine. Here is what cica is, where the trend came from, and how to use a cica cream in India's very different seasons.

What is centella asiatica (cica)?

Centella asiatica is a small creeping plant that grows in damp, warm places across Asia, including India. You may know it as gotu kola, or as mandukaparni in traditional Indian use. "Cica" is simply the short name the beauty world gave it, and cica creams are now easy to find in India too.

On a label you may see centella asiatica extract, or names of its well-known plant compounds such as madecassoside, asiaticoside and asiatic acid. In creams it is used to help skin feel calm, comfortable and cared for, and to support a healthy-looking skin barrier.

Why Korean skincare made cica famous

Korean skincare focuses on gentle layers, hydration and keeping the skin barrier happy, rather than scrubbing hard. Cica fits that idea perfectly. Korean brands popularised cica creams, balms and toners as the "calming step" in a routine, especially for skin that looks red after sun, shaving, masks or a new active.

How to use a cica cream

  1. Patch test. Apply a little behind the ear or on the jawline for 24 to 48 hours. Plant extracts can still bother some people.
  2. Cleanse gently. Use a mild face wash. Hard water in many Indian cities can leave skin feeling tight, so do not over-wash.
  3. Apply on slightly damp skin. This helps the cream spread and feel more hydrating.
  4. Use a pea-sized amount. Dot it on the forehead, cheeks, nose and chin and spread upwards.
  5. Night: make it your last step, or put it under a thicker cream if your skin is dry.
  6. Morning: a thin layer is fine, then always finish with sunscreen.

Centella in summer, monsoon and winter

Summer

Heat and sweat can make skin feel prickly and look flushed. Use a light cica gel-cream at night and a gel sunscreen in the day. Keep a cleansing routine simple: one gentle wash at night is usually enough.

Monsoon

Humidity makes heavy creams feel sticky. A thin layer of a light centella cream is often all you need. Our guide to building a simple night skincare routine helps you keep things light.

Winter and AC

Dry air pulls water out of the skin. Pair cica with a hydrating ingredient like hyaluronic acid, and seal it with a richer cream if your cheeks feel tight. People with dry skin may like our guide to avocado cream for dry skin.

Centella with other ingredients

Cica is a team player. It sits comfortably next to most common skincare ingredients:

  • Niacinamide: for smoother-looking texture and the look of smaller pores.
  • Hyaluronic acid: for hydration and a plump, dewy look.
  • Glutathione and alpha arbutin: for a more even-looking tone, with cica adding comfort.
  • Stronger actives (acids, retinoids): use cica on the same night or on rest nights for comfort, but do not stack several strong actives at once.

What a cica cream will not do

  • It will not replace sunscreen.
  • It is not a medicine for acne, eczema, rashes or infections.
  • It will not change your natural skin colour.
  • It cannot fix a harsh routine. If you scrub hard or use too many actives, cut back first.

See a dermatologist if your skin stays red, itchy or painful, if you have painful or cystic acne, a spreading rash, or if you are pregnant and unsure about a product.

Short answers

What is cica cream used for? A cica cream is used to help skin feel calm, comfortable and moisturised, especially after sun, heat or a strong active.

Is centella cream good for oily skin? Yes, in a light gel-cream texture. Use a thin layer, especially in summer and monsoon.

Can I use a cica cream every day? Yes, morning and night after a patch test. In the morning, finish with sunscreen.

Is cica cream good for sensitive skin? It is often chosen for sensitive skin, but patch test first, since any plant extract can bother some people.

Can men use centella cream? Yes. It suits men too, including after shaving.

This is general skincare information, not medical advice. Patch test any new product, and see a dermatologist if you have a skin condition.
