The best kojic acid cream in India is one that lists kojic acid clearly, uses it at a sensible level (creams typically contain about 1–2%), sits in a moisturising base with gentle partners like niacinamide or aloe vera, and comes in a sealed, properly labelled pack. Just as important: it only works for you if you are ready to wear sunscreen every single morning.

This is a buyer's guide, not a ranking. It shows you how to read a label, who should skip kojic acid, and how to start without irritating your skin.

What kojic acid does, and what strength to look for

Kojic acid is made by certain fungi during fermentation and has been used in skincare for decades. In a cream it helps reduce the look of dark spots, tan and uneven-looking tone over time. It works slowly. Skin renews roughly every 4–6 weeks, so a more even look usually takes 8–12 weeks of regular use.

  • Strength: about 1–2% is the usual range in creams. Higher is not better; it mainly means more chance of redness and stinging. Many brands do not print the percentage, which is common and not by itself a red flag.
  • Form: you may see "kojic acid" or "kojic dipalmitate". The second is a more stable form often described as gentler.
  • Stability: kojic acid does not like light and air. Opaque, airtight packs are better than clear jars, and a cream that has turned noticeably darker since you opened it may have gone off.

Irritation and sun sensitivity: the two real risks

Kojic acid suits most people, but two things catch beginners out.

Irritation

Some people get redness, dryness, itching or a stinging feeling, especially with sensitive skin or when they use too much. A pea-sized amount covers the whole face. If your skin feels hot or tight, cut back to two or three nights a week. If it burns, or you get a rash, stop. More detail in kojic acid side effects.

Sun

Freshly renewed skin is more easily bothered by sun, and sun exposure is the main reason dark spots come back. Without sunscreen, a kojic acid cream is mostly wasted money. Use a broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher every morning, about two fingers' worth for face and neck, and reapply if you are outdoors for long. That includes cloudy monsoon days and scooter rides to work.

Patch test before your first night

  1. Put a small dab on your inner arm or behind your ear.
  2. Leave it for 24–48 hours without washing that spot.
  3. Mild warmth that fades is usually fine. Redness, bumps or itching that stays means this cream is not for you.
  4. If the test is clear, start on your face every other night for two weeks, then move to nightly.
  5. Add only one new active product at a time. Our guide to introducing active ingredients explains the spacing.

Who should avoid kojic acid, or wait

  • Very sensitive skin, or skin prone to eczema or rosacea-like redness.
  • Sunburnt, broken or freshly peeled skin.
  • Skin that was waxed, threaded or bleached in the last 24–48 hours. Use a plain soothing moisturiser instead; see night cream after waxing and threading.
  • Anyone pregnant or breastfeeding: ask your doctor first.
  • Anyone already using a prescription cream for pigmentation or acne: ask your dermatologist before adding kojic acid.
  • Patches that may be melasma, or spots that are spreading or changing: see a dermatologist.
  • Anyone who will not wear sunscreen daily.

Choosing by skin type and budget

  • Oily or combination skin: a lighter cream with niacinamide; a thin layer at night is enough in humid weather. Niacinamide and kojic acid work well together, as explained in niacinamide and kojic acid together.
  • Dry skin: a richer base, such as avocado, makes kojic acid more comfortable.
  • Sensitive skin: start with a small jar and two or three nights a week.

Short answers

What is the best kojic acid cream in India? The one that suits your skin type, lists kojic acid clearly, makes honest claims and is properly sealed and labelled. Use it with sunscreen every morning.

What percentage of kojic acid is good in a cream? Creams typically contain about 1–2%. A higher number mostly raises the chance of irritation.

Can I use kojic acid cream every night? Yes, once your skin is used to it. Start every other night for two weeks.

Does kojic acid make skin sensitive to the sun? Sun can undo its work and bother freshly renewed skin, so daily SPF 30 or higher is essential.

How long before dark spots look lighter? Usually 8–12 weeks of regular use; older, deeper-looking marks can take longer.

This is general skincare information, not medical advice. Patch test any new product, and see a dermatologist if you have a skin condition.
