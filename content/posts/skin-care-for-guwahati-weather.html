Skin care for Guwahati weather means a light routine for most of the year, because the city is warm and humid from spring to autumn with a long, heavy monsoon, and then a short, dry, dusty winter. A gentle face wash, a thin moisturiser, broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning and a night cream that you use less of in the rainy months and more of in winter suit most skin in Guwahati.

Guwahati's year is different from that of the big cities on the plains. The rain starts earlier, stays longer, and winter brings dry air and dust rather than bitter cold. This guide gives you one simple routine and shows what to change in each season.

A simple daily routine for Guwahati skin

Morning

  1. Gentle face wash with lukewarm or cool water. Twice a day is enough, even in sticky weather.
  2. A thin moisturiser. Oily skin can use very little in the monsoon; dry skin needs a little more in winter. Our Avocado Glow Cream 15 g (₹399) has only avocado and aloe vera and no kojic acid, so it works as a plain moisturiser by day or night.
  3. Broad-spectrum sunscreen SPF 30 or higher, about two finger-lengths for face and neck, including cloudy and rainy days.

Night

  1. Cleanse to wash off sweat, sunscreen and road dust.

Guwahati's long monsoon

The northeast gets its monsoon early, and in Guwahati the rainy, damp weeks can run from late May well into September. The problem is usually skin that never fully dries, damp clothes and damp towels, not the rain itself.

  • Dry your face, neck and skin folds fully after getting wet. Pat, do not rub.
  • Wash towels often and dry them in the sun when you can; use a separate small towel for your face.
  • Change out of wet socks, shoes and clothes soon after reaching home.
  • If you have walked through flood or drain water, wash your feet and legs with soap and clean water and dry them well. See a doctor for any cut, wound or rash after that.
  • Keep the night cream thin and mainly on the cheeks if your T-zone is oily.

Guwahati summer: humid heat and strong sun

Before and between the rains, afternoons are hot and sticky, and the sun is strong. Sweat sits on the skin and tan builds up quickly on the face, neck and arms.

  • Wear loose cotton clothes and carry a handkerchief to blot sweat instead of rubbing.
  • Reapply sunscreen after lunch and after wiping your face.
  • Use gel-cream or light lotion textures rather than thick creams.
  • Skip scrubs on hot, irritated skin.

Guwahati winter: dry air and dust

From November to February, humidity drops, mornings are chilly, and dust from roads and construction is more noticeable. Skin can switch from oily to dry in a few weeks.

  • Moisturise within a few minutes of washing, while skin is still slightly damp.
  • Bathe in warm, not very hot, water.
  • Rinse off dust with lukewarm water in the evening; a gentle cleanse is enough.
  • Use a full pea-sized night cream on dry cheeks and around the nose.
  • Keep sunscreen on; winter days in Guwahati are often bright and sunny.

Bihu, Puja and wedding nights

Magh Bihu in January brings bonfires and feasts, Rongali Bihu in April brings dancing in the heat, and Durga Puja nights in October run late. Before a festival, a calm routine works better than trying new products: gentle cleanse, moisturiser, sunscreen by day and enough sleep. Do not try a new cream, facial or scrub in the week before a big day. Take off makeup gently before bed; our makeup removal guide shows how. Keep a little distance from bonfire smoke and heat, which dry the skin.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for an itchy rash in skin folds that spreads, ring-shaped or scaly patches, painful or cystic acne, wounds or rashes after walking through flood water, or patches of colour change that keep growing. Damp weather can make fungal skin problems more common, and those need a doctor rather than more face cream.

Short answers

What is the best skin care routine for Guwahati weather? A gentle face wash, a thin moisturiser, broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning and a night cream. Use less cream in the humid months and more in the dry winter.

Do I need sunscreen in the Guwahati monsoon? Yes. Daylight reaches your skin through clouds, so use SPF 30 or higher every morning.

Why does my skin feel dry in Guwahati in winter? The air becomes much drier and dustier from November to February. Moisturise on damp skin and keep bath water warm, not hot.

How much night cream should I use in Guwahati? About half a pea-sized amount in the monsoon for oily skin and a full pea-sized amount in winter.

Is Guwahati weather more like Kolkata or Delhi? It is closer to Kolkata, humid for much of the year, but with an earlier monsoon and a drier, dustier winter. See our Kolkata weather guide to compare.
