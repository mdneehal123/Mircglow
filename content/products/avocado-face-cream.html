How to Use This Avocado Face Cream

Use a small amount as the moisturising step in your daily skincare routine. The texture is designed to be easy to work into a simple routine.

  1. Cleanse and gently pat the face and neck dry.
  2. Take a small amount of cream with clean fingertips.
  3. Massage over the face and neck using gentle upward, outward motions.
  4. In the morning, follow with sunscreen; in the evening, use as the final moisturising step.

Start with a small amount and adjust to your preferred finish. If you are introducing a new cosmetic product, patch test first and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Using this avocado face cream under sunscreen

This is the lightest cream in our range, which makes it the one to use in the daytime. A simple morning routine looks like this.

  1. Rinse your face or cleanse lightly.
  2. Apply a small amount of Avocado Glow Nourishing Skin Cream.
  3. Wait a minute for it to settle.
  4. Apply sunscreen.

In humid weather, a very thin layer is enough. In winter or in air-conditioned rooms, you can use a little more.

Product FAQs

What is Avocado Glow Nourishing Skin Cream?

It is a 10g avocado face cream from Mirc Glow, formulated for nourishing, daily moisturising care and a soft-looking skin finish.

How do I use this avocado face cream?

Apply a small amount to clean, dry face and neck, then massage gently until absorbed. Use before sunscreen in the morning or as the final moisturising step at night.

Can I use it every day?

Yes, it is designed for daily cosmetic moisturising care. Patch test first when trying any new skincare product, particularly if your skin is sensitive.

Can men use this avocado face cream?

Yes. It is suitable for both women and men.

Can I wear it under make-up?

Yes. Apply a thin layer, let it settle for a minute, then continue with sunscreen and make-up.
