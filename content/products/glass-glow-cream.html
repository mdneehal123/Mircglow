How to use Glass Glow Cream

  1. Wash your face with a gentle cleanser and pat dry.
  2. If you use a serum or toner, apply it first and let it settle.
  3. Take a pea-sized amount and dot it on the forehead, cheeks, nose and chin.
  4. Spread gently upwards over the face and neck until it is absorbed.
  5. Use it at night as your last step. If you also use it in the morning, always finish with sunscreen.

Patch test behind the ear or on the jawline for 24 to 48 hours before the first full use. Most people notice softer, more hydrated skin within days; a more even-looking tone usually takes 8 to 12 weeks of regular use with sunscreen every day.

Which skin types is it for?

Dull or uneven-looking skin: the main reason people choose it.

Normal, combination and oily skin: a pea-sized amount is enough; the texture is light.

Dry skin: hyaluronic acid helps, and you can layer a richer cream such as our Avocado Night Cream on very dry nights.

Sensitive skin: start every second night after a patch test.

What to use it with, and what to keep apart

  • Fine together: a gentle cleanser, a hydrating toner or serum before it, and a richer cream such as the Avocado Night Cream on top of dry areas in winter.
  • Keep to different nights: retinol, exfoliating acids (AHA/BHA) and peels. Glass Glow Cream already has niacinamide and alpha arbutin, so you do not need extra brightening serums with it.
  • Skip for 24 to 48 hours: freshly waxed, threaded or bleached skin. Use a plain moisturiser such as the Avocado Glow Cream 15g (avocado and aloe vera only) for those days. See night cream after waxing or threading.
  • Every morning: a broad-spectrum sunscreen. Without it, no cream can keep spots from looking darker.

Glass Glow Cream in Indian weather

Summer and monsoon: half a pea-sized amount is enough on oily or combination skin; let it settle for a few minutes before bed. Winter and AC rooms: use the full amount, include the neck, and add a thin layer of a richer cream on dry cheeks if they still feel tight.
