What is glass skin?

Glass skin is not a skin colour and it is not make-up. It is a look: smooth texture, even-looking tone and a healthy, dewy finish. The idea comes from K-beauty, the Korean approach to skincare, which values hydration and patience over harsh quick fixes.

Every skin tone can have a glass skin look. What it needs is consistent moisture, gentle care and daily sun protection.

How to use the Korean glass skin kit

Morning routine

  1. Rinse or cleanse lightly.
  2. Apply a small amount of Avocado Glow Nourishing Skin Cream.
  3. Finish with a broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher.

Night routine

  1. Cleanse. Use an oil cleanser first if you wore sunscreen or make-up, then a gentle face wash.
  2. Pat in a hydrating toner, if you use one.
  3. Apply your night cream as the last step.

Which night cream, when?

Start by alternating. Use the Glutathione Cream one night and the Avocado Korean Night Cream the next. Both contain kojic acid, so alternating is the gentle way to begin.

Once your skin is comfortable, you can layer them on the same evening: a thin layer of Glutathione Cream first, then a thin layer of Avocado Korean Night Cream to seal it in.

Adjusting for Indian weather

Korean routines were designed for cold, dry winters. In India, use thinner layers in summer and during the monsoon, and fuller amounts in winter. Sunscreen is essential all year. Our article on the glass skin routine for Indian skin explains this in detail.
