MircGlow Avocado Korean Night Cream is a cosmetic moisturising cream made for evening use. Its creamy texture helps leave skin feeling comfortable overnight, while the formula supports a brighter, smoother and more even-looking complexion. It is not intended to treat medical skin conditions.

For customers building a simple Korean-inspired routine, this night cream for glowing skin fits after cleansing and any lightweight leave-on products. It is designed for adults who want nourishing, brightening-focused skincare without overstating what a cosmetic product can do.

Key ingredients in this avocado night cream

Avocado is valued in skincare for its nourishing, conditioning feel. Aloe vera is widely used in cosmetic formulas that aim to leave skin feeling hydrated and comfortable. Together, they make this glutathione-free avocado cream a practical choice for an overnight moisturising step.

Vitamin B3, also called niacinamide, is a familiar ingredient in Vitamin B3 skincare. Kojic Acid is commonly included in cosmetic brightening formulas. How to use Avocado Korean Night Cream

  1. Cleanse your face with a gentle cleanser and pat dry.
  2. Apply any lightweight products you already use in your evening routine.
  3. Take a small amount of MircGlow Avocado Korean Night Cream.
  4. Massage an even layer over the face, avoiding the immediate eye area.
  5. Leave on overnight and follow your usual morning routine.

Use this night cream in the evening. If your routine includes brightening ingredients, use broad-spectrum sunscreen during the day. Start gradually if you have sensitive skin or use several active skincare products.

How to use it for your skin type

Dry skin. Apply a full pea-sized amount to slightly damp skin.

Oily skin. Use half that amount, mainly on the cheeks. Our guide to night cream for oily skin explains why a thin layer works best.

Combination skin. Apply more on the cheeks and less on the forehead and nose.

Sensitive skin. Patch test first, and start with every second night for two weeks.

The step-by-step method is in how to apply night cream.

Using it in Indian weather

Korean creams were designed for cold, dry winters. In India, use a thinner layer in summer and during the monsoon, and the full amount in winter. If you sleep in an air-conditioned room, keep using it all year, because cooled air dries the skin overnight.
