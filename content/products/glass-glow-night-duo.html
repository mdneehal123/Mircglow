The Glass Glow Night Duo pairs our two most-loved evening creams: the Glass Glow Cream with glutathione, niacinamide, hyaluronic acid, centella asiatica and alpha arbutin, and the rich Avocado Night Cream with avocado, aloe vera, vitamin B3 and kojic acid. One works on a radiant, even-looking glass-skin look; the other keeps skin soft and comfortable. Bought together, the pair costs less than buying the two jars separately.

How to use the two creams

  1. Week 1: patch test each cream separately for 24 to 48 hours, a few days apart.
  2. Weeks 2 to 4: alternate nights: Glass Glow Cream one night, Avocado Night Cream the next.
  3. After that: on dry nights, apply a pea-sized amount of Glass Glow Cream first, wait two minutes, then a thin layer of Avocado Night Cream on dry areas.
  4. Every morning: wash your face and apply sunscreen. This matters with alpha arbutin and kojic acid.

Who is the duo for?

  • Dull or uneven-looking skin that also feels dry
  • Anyone who works in air-conditioned rooms all day
  • People who want a simple two-step Korean-style night routine
  • Oily skin: use Glass Glow Cream most nights and the avocado cream only on very dry areas

Product FAQs

Can I use both creams on the same night?

Yes, once your skin is used to both. Apply Glass Glow Cream first, then a thin layer of Avocado Night Cream on dry areas.

How long does the duo last?

About two months when you alternate the jars, a little less if you layer both every night.

Do the creams change natural skin colour?

No. They help skin look smoother, more radiant and more even, but they do not change your natural skin tone.

Can I buy the creams separately?

Yes: the Glass Glow Cream and the Avocado Night Cream are each sold on their own.

These are cosmetic creams, not medicines. See a dermatologist for any skin condition.
