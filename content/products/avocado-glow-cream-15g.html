What it looks like

The cream is pale green from the avocado, with a soft, whipped texture.

Avocado and aloe vera

Avocado is rich and comforting, which is why it is loved in creams for dry and tight-feeling skin. Aloe vera adds a light, soothing feel, so the cream stays comfortable even in warm weather. Together they make a straightforward moisturiser focused on softness and comfort. Read more on our ingredients page.

How to use

  1. Wash your face with a gentle cleanser and pat dry.
  2. Take a pea-sized amount with clean fingers.
  3. Dot it on the forehead, cheeks, nose and chin, then spread gently upwards over the face and neck.
  4. In the morning, finish with sunscreen. At night, use it as your last step.

Patch test behind the ear for 24 hours before using any new cream on your face.

Which skin types is it for?

Dry and normal skin. Use it morning and night.

Combination skin. Apply mainly to the cheeks, with a thin layer on the T-zone.

Oily skin. Use a very thin layer, or only at night.

Sensitive skin. With only avocado and aloe vera as key ingredients, it is our simplest cream. Patch test first all the same.

Product FAQs

What is in Avocado Glow Cream 15g?

Its key ingredients are avocado and aloe vera. It does not contain kojic acid or niacinamide. The full ingredient list is printed on the pack.

Can I use it every day?

Yes. It is a daily moisturiser that you can use in the morning, at night, or both.

Can I use it in the daytime?

Yes. Apply a thin layer and put sunscreen on top, as you would with any moisturiser.

Is it good for oily skin?

Use a very thin layer, or only at night. Oily skin usually needs less cream than dry skin.

Does it change skin colour?

No. It moisturises skin so it looks softer, smoother and fresher. It does not change your natural skin tone.

How long does a 15 g jar last?

About 3 to 4 weeks of daily use at a pea-sized amount.

Can men use it?

Yes. It suits women and men.

This is a cosmetic moisturiser, not a medicine. See a dermatologist for any skin condition.
