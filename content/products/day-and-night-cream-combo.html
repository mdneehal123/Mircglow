The Mirc Glow day and night cream combo gives you a complete moisturising routine in two jars. In the morning you use the light Avocado Glow Nourishing Skin Cream. In the evening you finish with the rich Avocado Night Cream with Kojic Acid. Bought together, the pair costs less than buying each cream separately.

It is made for anyone who wants soft, comfortable skin without a shelf full of products.

What is in the combo

1. Avocado Glow Nourishing Skin Cream, 10 g (day). A small jar of avocado-enriched moisturiser for daily use. It is light enough to sit under sunscreen and make-up, and the size fits easily in a handbag or travel pouch.

2. Avocado Night Cream with Kojic Acid, 30 g (night). A richer cream with avocado, aloe vera, vitamin B3 and kojic acid. It is the last step of your evening and works while you sleep.

Why a day and night cream combo makes sense

Your skin needs different things at different times. In the day it faces sun, heat and dust, so it needs a light layer that will not feel heavy under sunscreen. At night there is no sun and no make-up, so a richer cream can stay on undisturbed for hours.

Using one cream for both jobs is a compromise. A light cream is not enough at night, and a rich cream feels greasy by noon. Two creams, each made for its own time of day, solve that.

Both creams in this combo are built around avocado, so they work together comfortably. Avocado oil is naturally high in oleic acid, which gives the creams their soft, cushioned feel.

What you get from using both

  • Skin that feels soft and comfortable from morning to night.
  • A light daytime layer that sits well under sunscreen.
  • A nourishing evening layer that eases the tight feeling after cleansing.
  • Support for a smoother, more even-looking tone from the vitamin B3 and kojic acid in the night cream.
  • A routine simple enough to keep up every day.

How to use the combo

Morning

  1. Wash your face with a gentle cleanser, or simply rinse.
  2. Apply a small amount of Avocado Glow Nourishing Skin Cream.
  3. Finish with a broad-spectrum sunscreen.

Night

  1. Cleanse your face. Cleanse twice if you wore sunscreen or make-up.
  2. Pat your skin dry, leaving it slightly damp.
  3. Press a pea-sized amount of Avocado Night Cream into your face and neck.

If kojic acid is new to your skin, use the night cream every second night for the first two weeks. Our guide shows how to apply night cream step by step.

Who is this combo for?

Dry and normal skin. This is the best match. Use the full amount of both creams.

Combination skin. Use both, but apply more on the cheeks and less on the forehead and nose.

Oily skin. Use a very small amount of the day cream, and half a pea of the night cream. See night cream for oily skin.

Sensitive skin. Patch test each cream before first use, and introduce them one at a time, a week apart.

The combo is suitable for both women and men.

Key ingredients

  • Avocado. In both creams. Rich and nourishing.
  • Aloe vera. In both creams. Keeps the texture fresh.
  • Vitamin B3. In both creams. Also called niacinamide, a favourite in Korean skincare for a smooth-looking surface.
  • Kojic acid. In both creams. Used in cosmetics for a brighter, more even-looking tone.

What to expect

Skin usually feels softer after the first night. Within a week, dry patches begin to settle. A smoother, more even-looking tone takes four to eight weeks of steady use with daily sunscreen. Results vary from person to person.

How long does the combo last?

The 10 g day cream is a trial and travel size and lasts around two to three weeks of daily use. The night cream lasts around four to six weeks at a pea-sized amount each night.

Good to know

  • Wear sunscreen every morning. This matters more while you use kojic acid.
  • Patch test before first use, and stop if irritation occurs.
  • Store the jars closed, away from heat and direct sunlight.
  • These are cosmetic products and are not intended to treat any skin condition.
