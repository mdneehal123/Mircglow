Key ingredients in this avocado night cream

The listed key ingredients are avocado, aloe vera, vitamin B3 and kojic acid. Here is what each one brings.

Avocado

The avocado fruit is naturally rich in oil. Avocado oil is high in oleic acid, which is why it feels so soft on the skin. In this cream it provides the comforting, cushioned layer that dry skin needs.

Aloe vera

Aloe vera is widely used in skincare for its light, fresh feel. It balances the richness of avocado so the cream does not sit heavily on your face.

Vitamin B3

Also called niacinamide, vitamin B3 is one of the most popular ingredients in Korean skincare for a smooth, refined-looking surface. It is gentle and suits most skin types.

Kojic acid

Kojic acid is used in cosmetics made for a brighter, more even-looking tone. Because of it, daily sunscreen is important while you use this cream.

Who is it for?

Dry skin. This is the skin type that enjoys it most. Use a full pea-sized amount, and go back over dry patches with a second thin layer.

Normal and combination skin. Apply a little more on the cheeks and less on the forehead and nose.

Oily skin. You can still use it. Take half the usual amount and keep it for the evening. More tips are in our guide to night cream for oily skin.

Sensitive skin. Patch test first, and begin with every second night.

The cream is suitable for both women and men.

How to use

  1. Cleanse your face and pat it dry, leaving the skin slightly damp.
  2. Take a pea-sized amount and warm it between your fingertips.
  3. Press it gently over your face and neck, avoiding the eye area.
  4. Use it as the final moisturising step of your evening routine.
  5. In the morning, finish your routine with sunscreen.

If kojic acid is new to your skin, start with every second night for two weeks and then move to nightly use. As with any cosmetic product, patch test before first use and stop if irritation occurs. Our step-by-step guide shows how to apply night cream properly.

Where it fits in a Korean-inspired routine

Korean routines move from the thinnest texture to the thickest, and the cream always comes last.

  1. Cleanse, twice if you wore sunscreen or make-up.
  2. Pat in a hydrating toner.
  3. Apply a serum, if you use one.
  4. Seal everything in with this avocado night cream.

What to expect

The first night. Skin feels softer and less tight the next morning.

After one week. Dry, flaky patches begin to settle, and skin looks more hydrated.

After four to eight weeks. With nightly use and daily sunscreen, skin tone looks smoother and more even.

Everyone's skin is different, so your results may come sooner or later than this. What matters most is using it consistently.

Using it through the seasons

In winter, when the air is dry, use the full amount every night and include your neck. In summer and during the monsoon, a thinner layer is enough. If you sleep in an air-conditioned room, keep using it all year, because cooled air dries the skin overnight.

Good to know before you start

  • Wear a broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning.
  • Add only one new product at a time, so you know how your skin responds.
  • Avoid using it on broken or irritated skin.
  • Close the jar tightly and store it away from heat and direct sunlight.
  • Check the use-before date printed on the pack.
