What is in the combo

1. Glutathione Cream with Vitamin C and Kojic Acid, 30 g. A premium evening cream for a hydrated, smooth and radiant-looking complexion.

Why pair these two creams?

They do different jobs. Glutathione is an antioxidant that your body makes naturally, and in skincare it is used in formulas designed for a fresh, luminous look. Avocado is rich in oil and gives a cushioned, nourishing layer that dry skin welcomes.

One brings radiance-focused care. The other brings comfort. Skin that is both well moisturised and even-looking is what people usually mean when they talk about a healthy glow.

Two ways to use this night cream combo

Option 1: Alternate nights

This is the gentler way, and the one we suggest you start with.

  • Night one: Glutathione Cream.
  • Night two: Avocado Night Cream.
  • Repeat.

Both creams contain kojic acid, so alternating gives your skin an easy introduction.

Option 2: Layer them

Once your skin is comfortable, you can use both on the same evening. Apply a thin layer of Glutathione Cream first, wait a minute, and then seal it with a thin layer of Avocado Night Cream. Use less of each than you would alone.

If your skin is sensitive, stay with option one.

Your evening routine, step by step

  1. Cleanse your face. Cleanse twice if you wore sunscreen or make-up.
  2. Pat dry, leaving skin slightly damp.
  3. Apply toner or serum, if you use them.
  4. Apply tonight's cream: a pea-sized amount, pressed gently into the face and neck.
  5. In the morning, finish your routine with sunscreen.

Results vary, and they depend on using the creams consistently and wearing sunscreen every day. More detail is in how long glutathione cream takes to work.

Good to know

  • Sunscreen every morning is essential while using kojic acid.
  • Patch test before first use, and stop if irritation occurs.
  • Do not combine with strong exfoliating acids or retinoids on the same night until your skin has settled.
  • Store the jars closed, away from heat and sunlight.
  • These are cosmetic products and are not intended to treat any skin condition.
