How to Use Glutathione Cream

  1. Cleanse your face and gently pat skin dry.
  2. Apply a small amount to the face and neck, avoiding the eye area.
  3. Massage with light upward and outward motions until absorbed.
  4. Use in the evening as your moisturising step; in the daytime, follow with broad-spectrum sunscreen.

Patch test before first use, especially if your skin is sensitive or you are introducing active ingredients. If discomfort occurs, stop use and seek advice from a qualified professional.

5 mistakes to avoid

  1. Skipping sunscreen in the morning. This is the most common reason people see no change.
  2. Applying it over unwashed skin.
  3. Using too much. A pea-sized amount covers the whole face.
  4. Stopping after two weeks.
  5. Adding several new products at once, so you cannot tell which one your skin likes.
